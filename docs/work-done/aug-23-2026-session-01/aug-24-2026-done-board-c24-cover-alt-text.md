## Ruling 1 — the cover is data-free. `kad-tajuk` stands

**Both seats arrived here independently, on different evidence, and the block is
sustained.**

The verification seat ruled **BLOCK** on figure-bearing covers for A1, A3, A4,
A5, A7 and A8, and found the generator brief's own table wrong in four of eight
rows. The headline is A5: the brief says the Perak cover shows *"Perak's
figure"*, and **Perak sets no rate.** The only Perak number in the draft is
RM101, which the article exists to debunk, and which this board already ruled on
23 Ogos may appear in prose only and *"never in a table cell, a heading, the H1
or the meta"*. A cover is more exposed than all four.

Two figures out of eight survive on the facts: Pahang RM100 and Negeri Sembilan
RM200/RM100, both grade A, both re-verified in session today.

**On the A6 carve-out, I side with the verification seat and decline it.** A
figure-bearing cover for A6 alone would be accurate. It would also make the
template data-bearing, and the next article that "looks safe" will be a grade C
one. The coverage seat proposed the same carve-out and then conceded it
explicitly — *"if the board would rather hold a clean no-figures line,
HK-C-0006 becomes `Kadar rasmi ditetapkan` and I do not fight it."* Concession
taken. **No cover in this batch carries a ringgit figure.**

**This ruling did not require overriding the generator brief.** The brief set its
own tie-break: *"If a draft and this table disagree, the draft wins and you tell
me."* Four rows disagree with their drafts. We are telling him.

The geometry reason from spec §7 stands unchanged and is arithmetic: 2464×700
down to 1920×2400 is a 4.4× ratio swing, and no type size survives it above the
28px floor. But the stronger reason is the verification seat's, and it is the one
I want on the record: **on these eight articles the qualification is the fact.**
The true answer is usually *belum disahkan* or *tiada ketetapan*, and a cover is
the one surface that strips qualification on its way into WhatsApp.

**Correction the board accepts against itself:** the coverage seat falsified
visual-asset strategy §2.3's claim that original graphics *"will not win an image
pack."* Ahrefs, `my`, 24 Ogos 2026: 13 of 13 cluster keywords carry image SERP
features; the image pack sits at **position 1 on `mas kahwin johor`** (1,000/mo),
above the AI Overview, and is occupied by typographic data cards. §2.3 is wrong
for this cluster and should be amended. It does not change ruling 1 — a title
card would not win that pack either, because it is the data winning, not the
graphic. It does change my own §0 cut, which is motion 1 below.

the answer, and the cards drop it to restate the keyword the skop line already

They are the same defect. The spec derived title lines as topic labels, and on a
"no rate" article a topic label reads as a claim that a rate exists.

**Sustained: HK-C-0004 and HK-C-0006 BLOCK** (coverage seat) — two cards, one
sentence, and the sentence was not the answer.

## Ruling 3 — the eight alt texts: REVISED, then PASSED

The packet's alt texts were accurate for the cards the spec specified. Once the
title lines changed, all eight had to be rewritten, which is why the packet was
right to hold the `/humanizer` pass until after the ruling.

The coverage seat cleared the alt texts of the charge the packet brought against
itself: the echo of the H1 is not keyword stuffing and does not breach style
guide §13.3. The inherited library's failure is *one target keyword repeated
across unrelated images*. These repeat a format noun and each names a different
jurisdiction. Different mechanism. The writer should not be marked down for a
resemblance that is only superficial.

**Simplification, required output of every review.** Three cuts, all carried:

1. **The five-word prefix `Kad tajuk artikel bagi …` cut to three.** The first
   words are what a screen-reader user hears; spending them on a format label
   wastes them.
2. **All quote marks removed.** The packet used curly quotes; the coverage seat
   kept them arguing straight quotes would break the YAML. Neither is needed — a
   screen reader announcing punctuation is noise, and removing them settles the
   YAML question rather than working around it.
3. **Entity doubling removed** on HK-C-0003 and HK-C-0005, which said Johor and
   Perak twice in eleven words.

## Recurring failure mode, for the brief rather than the review

Both blocking findings came from the same root: **a topic label was used where an
answer belonged.** The spec wrote title lines as subject headings, which is
correct for a card on a page that sets a rate and false on a page that finds
there is none. Six of the eight C2.4 articles answer *tiada* or *belum disahkan*,
and the visual layer was specified as though they all answered with a number.

**Fix upstream:** any brief that specifies a card, a heading or a label for an
article must take the wording from that article's H1 answer half, not from its
keyword. Added to the kit spec §4 as a label convention so the next kit does not
repeat it.
