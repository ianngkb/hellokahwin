## What was done

Task 5B, the cluster launch plan the board actually asked for. Strategy only:
no articles, drafts, outlines, titles, slugs or meta descriptions were
written, per the brief's hard rule.

**Extended the topical map from 25 clusters to 26, and changed its shape.**
The Phase 1 framework tables held 25 clusters, not the 21 recorded in the
Phase 1 log; that miscount is corrected in the new document. Six Phase 1
clusters were merged away for failing the eight-article floor, and seven were
added, five of which make up an entirely new pillar.

**Found a competitor the Phase 1 audit missed, and it changes the picture.**
`ppsignature.com`, a bridal boutique at Domain Rating 4, holds 2,263 Malaysian
organic keywords, 1,493 of them in the top three, and roughly 29,745 organic
visits a month. That is more than nikahsatu.com and TheWeddingNotebook.com
combined. `songketdunia.my` at DR 3 was also resized: 823 keywords, 505
top-three, 10,107 visits a month, where Phase 1 had recorded only a single
page at 593 visits.

**Pulled the SERP evidence the board asked for.** Twenty-two new SERP
Overviews, one per cluster head, recording who ranks on page one, at what
Domain Rating, with what page type and what traffic. Three volume-history
series were pulled to test seasonality claims rather than assume them.

**Ran sixteen new Malay keyword batches** on entities the Phase 1 research had
not touched, which is where the new pillar came from.

**Named the rejects with data.** Roughly 75,000 monthly searches were
deliberately excluded, each with the number that produced the decision,
including three that are provably winnable and were passed on anyway.

**Did the click arithmetic against the 1,500 clicks/28d target** and gave a
plain verdict rather than a reassurance.

The document was drafted, run through /humanizer, then re-checked. The
humanizer pass found 369 em dashes against zero in all three Phase 1
documents, and the whole document was rewritten to match the established
voice. Final check confirms zero em dashes, zero en dashes, zero curly quotes
and no clustered AI vocabulary.

## What it changed

**The map got a new pillar worth roughly 32,000 searches a month.** P7,
covering jodoh, taaruf, merisik, cincin and pertunangan, is entirely new. It
sits before the moment a couple would normally visit a wedding site, which is
why nobody has built it, and it feeds directly into the hantaran pillar we
already have pages in.

**The competitive brief for every future report changed.** ppsignature.com
belongs in the competitor set and nikahsatu is no longer the benchmark.

**The 1,500-click target now has arithmetic behind it and a stated risk.** The
demand supports it at 1.04% capture of the mapped set. The calendar is the
constraint, and the honest fallback if the two writers are not approved is
roughly 45 articles with a proportionally lower click target.

**Four clusters are explicitly downgraded on evidence.** Inai, baju nikah, kad
kahwin and the government-owned procedure heads all carry large volume that an
article cannot capture. Saying so now prevents 80 hours of work landing on
image packs and registration portals.
