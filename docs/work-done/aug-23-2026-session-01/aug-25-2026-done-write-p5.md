## The bunting pelamin correction

The brief commissioned a possible fourth candidate on `bunting pelamin` (800),
`maksud bunting pelamin` (350) and `bunting pelamin maksud` (300), on the
launch plan's premise that these are 1,450 searches a month for "the meaning of a
decoration, with nobody explaining it."

**Both halves of that premise are wrong, and the article was not written.**

`bunting pelamin` does not name a decoration. *Bunting* here means pregnant. The
term describes a bride who is found to be pregnant very soon after the wedding,
and the whole search interest is about how pregnancy is dated from the last
menstrual period rather than from the wedding night.

It is also not unanswered. Page one carries Free Malaysia Today (2 April 2019),
`motherhood.com.my`, `majalahpama.my` and `ecentral.my`, all answering exactly
that question, some with pregnancy-dating explainers.

Evidence gathered 25 Ogos 2026:

- Free Malaysia Today, "Bunting pelamin antara mitos dan fakta perlu diketahui",
  2 April 2019 — defines it as a misnomer for a woman pregnant shortly after the
  ceremony, and explains the last-menstrual-period calculation. No named doctor
  is quoted in the piece.
- `ecentral.my/bunting-pelamin/` — titled "Cara Pengiraan Usia Hamil & Tanda
  Waktu Subur".
- `story.motherhood.com.my/my/bunting-pelamin/` and two `majalahpama.my` pieces,
  all pregnancy-dating content.
- Searched separately for a printed backdrop sense. Malaysian printers use
  *bunting* for a vertical banner and none of them sell a "bunting pelamin"
  product. The string does not exist in that trade.

**Recommendation to head-of-seo-content:** move these three keywords out of C5.1.
They are pregnancy and post-wedding content, they belong to
`motherhood.com.my`'s entity rather than ours, and under §1.2 rejection criterion
5 they are arguably out of the map entirely. The launch plan's C5.1 topic 4
should be struck or rewritten.

## Two findings worth repeating

1. **Card printers publish almost no prices.** ExpressPrint and Printoka both
   price by configurator. Printlab and Murah Print publish nothing on their
   `kad kahwin` pages. Nurfa Grafik advertises "serendah RM45" without stating
   the quantity it covers. The only flat published card price found anywhere was
   a digital card at RM59.90. Stated in the article as the finding it is.
2. **The written record on bunga telur is thinner than it looks.** The BM
   Wikipedia article and the UPM i-PUTRA article carry near-identical text, so
   they are one source, not two. The Hindu-influence origin claim traces to a
   single UiTM student project (Zalina Husain, Fakulti Seni Lukis dan Seni Reka,
   AD110). The article says so rather than laundering it into settled history.

## What the absence costs, stated plainly

`pelamin` is 1,500 searches a month at KD 0 with no article on page one, against
Pinterest boards, an Instagram profile and Shutterstock. We are entering an image
SERP with typography. The article will still be the best-sourced page on the term

will not be the most attractive one, and on this particular keyword that gap is
worth naming rather than absorbing.
