## The four blocks

### B1 — Selangor's minimum marriage age has been 18/18 since 18 March 2019

**Article:** `syarat-sah-nikah.md`, section *Umur*.

**What it said.** That section 8 sets eighteen for men and sixteen for women,
that a 2018 amendment Bill would replace it with eighteen for both, and that
"kami tidak dapat mengesahkan pemberitahuan itu daripada Warta."

**What the source says.** The amendment is not a Bill; it is Enakmen A56, with
Royal Assent 10 October 2018 and gazetted 18 October 2018. Section 2 substitutes
"jika lelaki atau perempuan itu berumur kurang daripada lapan belas tahun". The
commencement notification exists: **Sel. P.U. 11, Warta Kerajaan Negeri
Selangor, Jil. 72 No. 7, 1 April 2019**, appointing **18 March 2019** as the
date the Enactment comes into operation. Section 3 also inserts section 8a, the
procedure for an under-18 application to the Court.

**Why it was a block.** A page whose subject is what makes a marriage valid was
stating a seven-year-superseded age as Selangor's current rule. The hedge
("sahkan dengan JAIS") does not repair it, because the sentence before it
asserts the old figure as what the enactment "menetapkan".

**Why the writer missed it.** They looked for the commencement notification on
gazette and JAKESS sources. It is not there. It is hosted by MAIS. The Dewan
Negeri Selangor section-8 page also still renders the unamended 2003 text, under
a disclaimer that it is general information only — so the one page that looks
authoritative is the one that is stale.

**Fixed by** rewriting the section to state 18/18, citing Enakmen A56 s.2 and
Sel. P.U. 11 with the commencement date, adding section 8a, and adding the
scoping line that most other states still set sixteen for women. Both gazettes
added to `## Sumber`.

### B2 — MP Sepang's rates and effective date were both wrong

**Articles:** `C6-2-A1`, section *Majlis Perbandaran Sepang*; `C6-2-A4`, rate
table and source note.

**What it said.** "Dewan orang ramai RM40 sejam tanpa penghawa dingin, RM80
sejam berhawa dingin, cagaran RM200 (RM500 di Bandar Baru Salak Tinggi).
Auditorium Taman Tasik Cyberpark RM300 sehari, cagaran RM1,000." And: "Kadar itu
tertulis berkuat kuasa 1 Julai 2016. Sepuluh tahun ialah tempoh yang panjang…"

**What the source says.** mpsepang.gov.my, Kemudahan Awam & Kadar Sewaan, live
today, marked **berkuat kuasa 1 Oktober 2022**. Upacara Perkahwinan, per jam:
dewan 1 & 2 gelanggang RM100 without air-conditioning, RM150 with; dewan 3
gelanggang RM200 and RM250. Cagaran RM500 for both. Dewan Serbaguna Kelab
Komuniti RM1,500 weekday / RM2,000 public holiday for a four-hour block,
cagaran RM500. Use for a wedding requires a written application and the Yang
Dipertua's comment or approval.

No RM40, no RM80, no RM200 deposit, no RM300-a-day auditorium, and the string
"2016" appears nowhere on the page. The Auditorium Taman Tasik Cyberpark appears
only as a phone-routing note.

**Why it was a block.** Not just wrong prices — A1 built an editorial argument on
them ("paling lama tidak dikemas kini", and step 6 of its own how-to-check list
told readers Sepang's published rates are from 2016). A reader following that
advice would budget RM80 an hour against a real RM150.

**Fixed by** rewriting A1's Sepang section from the live rate card, correcting
step 6 of the checklist, and correcting A4's table row and source note.

### B3 — MBSA does publish hall rates, and they are exactly the ones the article's target query wants

**What it said.** "Majlis Bandaraya Shah Alam tidak menyiarkan senarai kadar

and did not check *Tempahan Dewan Bankuet*, its sibling page under the same
menu.

**Fixed by** rewriting A3's Shah Alam section around the published rate card,
including the comparison it makes possible (MBSA's four included setup hours
against MBPJ's RM600-an-hour setup charge), and narrowing the "not published"
claim to the other MBSA halls, which is still true. A1's paragraph narrowed the
same way, with its own source line.

   masa:" opens a paragraph in `borang-nikah.md` and again in `C6-2-A1` — two

   `cover-borang-nikah.png`; P6 uses `./C6-2-A1-…-cover.png` with a leading
   `./`. One of the two conventions is probably wrong for the parser. For the

## Method note for the next run

Two of the four blocks were only visible because the rate card or the schedule
was read by **word x-coordinate** rather than as flat text. `pdftotext -layout`
silently misaligns fee columns in these documents — on the Perak Jadual Kedua it
produced a table that would have made "Kebenaran Berkahwin" RM10 instead of RM5,
and the writer, correctly distrusting it, marked the schedule unextractable and
worked around it. The coordinate read recovers it cleanly.

**Any future price or fee taken from a government PDF should be read by
coordinate, and the reading stated in the work log.** Flat text extraction of a
multi-column rate card is not evidence.
