## The metadata deadline defect, measured

`generateMetadata` returns `{}` on a 1.5s deadline miss and Next falls back to
the root layout title. **That empty result is cached.** After the batch, six of
seven articles served `HelloKahwin — Idea & Panduan Perkahwinan Malaysia` with
a correct `<h1>` on the same page.

This run's data narrows the cause. A peer session had attributed it to
concurrency; **this sweep was strictly sequential, 4 s apart, and still saw it**,
on responses whose own headers read `cache=MISS, age=0`. Cold render times here
were **1.1 s warm and 3.5 to 6.3 s cold**, so the deadline is missed on a
genuinely cold render whether or not anything else is in flight.
`tempat-beli-hantaran` returned **504** on its first cold render, twice, and
200 on the second.

Repair, and it is reliable: **purge, request once to warm, purge again, request
again.** Two rounds fixed all seven, every time, sequentially
(`EVIDENCE/heal-titles.log`). Every purge in this run was one path at a time;
no bulk purge was issued.

The practical consequence for the next publish: **a purge re-arms this defect**,
so the warm-then-purge cycle belongs after the last purge of a batch, not
before.

## A site-wide gap this run found

**No article on the site emits `FAQPage` schema.** Checked on all seven of this
batch and on `nisbah-hantaran` from CONT-08: zero. Every writer is told to shape
a `Soalan lazim` block "marked up as FAQ schema by the engineer at ingest" and
nothing does the marking up.

Re-measured across all seven of this batch's rendered pages, counting nested
`@type` rather than top-level only, the article template emits six types:
`Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `ImageObject`, `ListItem`, `Organization`,
`WebPage`. SEO-05's site-wide sweep adds `ItemList`, which appears on listing
pages rather than article pages. `FAQPage` is absent under either extraction.

SEO-05 sized it the same evening on a sequential sweep of all 69 published
articles: **31 carry the block, 0 emit the schema.** One of the 31 is
`mas-kahwin-ikut-negeri`. Owner `full-stack-engineer`; evidence at
`docs/work-done/2026-08-27-seo-05-titles-EVIDENCE/faq-schema-gap.json`.

**It is one fix, not 31.** CONT-05's framing, verified here against the stored
TipTap of all seven of this batch: every FAQ question is already an `h3` under
the `## Soalan lazim` `h2`, four of them per article, with no other heading
level inside the block. The content is already correct and already uniform, so
this is a renderer change over existing rows and nothing editorial moves.
