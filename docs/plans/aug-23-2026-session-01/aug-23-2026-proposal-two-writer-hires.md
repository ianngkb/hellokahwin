# Hiring Proposal — Two Malay Content Writers

**Status:** DRAFT — awaiting board approval
**From:** ceo-hellokahwin · **Date:** 23 Aug 2026

**Depends on:** approved Cluster Launch Plan (26 clusters, 204 topics)

---

## The ask in one line

Two Malay content writers, hired via /raiseagents (one invocation each),
**split by discipline rather than by volume** — because the 204 mapped topics
are two genuinely different kinds of writing, and one of them carries real
risk if done badly.

## Why two, and why now

The 90-day goal is 80 articles: **6 new articles plus 1 upgrade per week for
13 weeks.** The Head of SEO & Content owns keyword strategy, article briefs
and quality control — the 21-point quality bar is the only thing standing
between bulk production and filler. If that person also drafts, they become
the bottleneck within a fortnight and QC is the first thing sacrificed.

Production volume is the binding constraint on the entire plan. Not keyword
difficulty, not competition — we established the market is uncontested at
difficulty 0–2. **If the writers are not approved, the honest restatement is
~45 articles and a proportionally lower click target**, which I would rather
put in front of you now than miss quietly in November.

## Why split by discipline, not just double up

The cluster map divides cleanly into two kinds of work that need different
instincts, and splitting this way also satisfies the method's rule that
writers work separate clusters to avoid keyword cannibalisation.

## Hire 1 — Malay Wedding Writer: Adat, Agama & Prosedur

**The authority track. This is where the volume and the risk both sit.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owns** | P1 Nikah & Undang-undang · P2 Hantaran & Mas Kahwin · P3 Ucapan, Doa & Adab Majlis · P7 Sebelum Nikah (jodoh, merisik, tunang) |
| **Demand covered** | Roughly 67,000 searches/month — the majority of the mapped set |
| **First clusters** | mas kahwin ikut negeri, hantaran kahwin, gubahan dulang hantaran (the three Tier 1 launches) |

**Why this role exists.** These clusters answer procedural and religious
questions: what the rukun nikah are, what mas kahwin is set at in Kelantan
versus Selangor, what goes in a dulang, what to say at an akad. Getting them
wrong is not a quality problem, it is a credibility problem — and the quality
bar already requires that *any religious or legal claim names its source and
its state.* This is also precisely where the incumbents are generic and we
can be specific, which is our entire overtaking mechanism.

**What they must be good at**
- Native Bahasa Melayu, culturally fluent in Malay-Muslim wedding practice —
  adat merisik, bertunang, akad nikah, berinai, sanding, kenduri, hantaran,
  mas kahwin, taklik.
- **Sourcing discipline**: cites JAKIM and state religious department sources,
  names which state's enactment a rule belongs to, and says "practice varies"
  rather than picking one. **Reports procedure; never issues fatwa.**
- Comfortable with state-by-state variance — fourteen different mas kahwin
  rates is a feature of the content, not an inconvenience.
- Writes to a structure: every sub-type gets its own heading, head question
  answered in the first 60 words.

**Hard rules built into the persona**: every claim sourced and dated; every
draft through /humanizer; no fatwa; no fabricated figures.

## Hire 2 — Malay Wedding Writer: Inspirasi, Vendor & Venue

**The inspiration and directory track. This is where the traffic model the
competitors actually use lives.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Owns** | P4 Busana & Penampilan · P5 Pelamin, Kad & Cenderahati · P6 Venue, Kos & Perancangan · the venue/vendor directory spine |
| **Demand covered** | Roughly 37,000 searches/month, plus the uncontested directory layer |
| **Strategic note** | Our single best page today is `/dewan-kahwin/` — a venue listicle earning 25 of our 32 clicks. Both competitors make most of their traffic the same way. This role owns that engine. |

**Why this role exists.** The audit found that venue and vendor entity pages
carry 40%+ of the winners' traffic, need few words, and rank quickly because
nobody has written a proper page about most venues. That is the highest
traffic-per-hour work available to us and it needs someone whose default is
data completeness — capacity numbers, price bands, addresses — not prose.
The inspiration clusters (pelamin, baju, solekan) need the opposite instinct:
aesthetic judgement and the ability to pair writing with visuals.

**What they must be good at**
- Native Bahasa Melayu with a feel for the aspirational register wedding
  readers actually want — warm and practical, never stiff.
- **Data-page discipline**: a directory entry is 250–500 words of verified
  facts. Getting a venue's capacity or price band wrong is the failure mode.
- Visual judgement — which images carry an article, how a listicle is
  structured so a reader who came for pictures leaves through a useful link.
- Verifies vendor and venue details rather than repeating marketing copy.

**Hard rules built into the persona**: no unverified capacity or price
claims; no stock photo of a non-Malay wedding on a page about adat; every
draft through /humanizer.

## How they work

Both report to `head-of-seo-content`, who writes the briefs and holds QC.
Neither publishes; drafts go through the quality bar and then to the
publishing path once it exists. Both work in **visible Orca terminals**,
dispatched per cluster, like every other delegated task.

**Cadence per writer:** 3 articles a week each, plus the SEO head's 1 upgrade
— clearing the 80-article goal with a buffer, because seven a week with no
slack means the first bad week is unrecoverable and recovery always comes out
of quality.

## What I am NOT proposing yet

A **visual content lead**. The visual asset strategy is still being produced —
we have no confirmed source for the 1,000+ images the plan requires, and the
rights status of the 682 inherited media items is unverified. If that work
concludes we need a dedicated role, I will bring it to you as a separate,
evidenced proposal rather than bundling it into this one.

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Two writers still can't hold 6/week | Weekly article count is reported at every meeting; a slip shows 30 days before it hits traffic |
| Religious/legal error damages credibility | Sourcing rules are hard rules in the persona; SEO head QC checks source and state on every claim |
| Directory entries repeat vendor marketing copy | Verification requirement in the persona; capacity and price bands must be checkable |
| Both writers drift into the same clusters | Cluster ownership is split by pillar; `parent_topic` deduplication is a standing rule |
