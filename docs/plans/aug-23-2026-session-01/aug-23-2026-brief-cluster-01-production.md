## What to do

### 1. Open the pillar (workflow steps P1, P3–P6)
- Confirm the cluster set, head keywords and topics against the approved
  cluster plan; **refresh the Ahrefs data if it is stale** — it is a day old,
  so a spot-check is enough unless something looks wrong.
- Ask `editorial-verification-lead` to open the **currency register** for
  this pillar: mas kahwin rates by state are the most volatile claims we
  will publish, and they need their sources recorded before anyone drafts.
- Ask `managing-editor` for the pillar's **voice notes** — and note that the
  style guide may not exist yet. **If it does not, the Managing Editor writes
  it first.** Nothing drafts before there is a voice to draft to; that was
  the whole reason that hire came first.
- Write the **article briefs** for the cluster's topics: target keyword with
  volume and difficulty, the questions each article must answer, internal
  links specified up front, supply lever.

### 2. Produce the articles (workflow stages 1–6)
Assign to `writer-adat-agama-prosedur` (this is the authority track). For each
article: brief → source before drafting → draft → **review board** →
humanizer → your SEO QC.

**Every article goes through `/bmad-party-mode`** with the four seats. This is
the board's explicit instruction. The room produces a revised article, not a
list of notes; the writer revises in session; the verification seat can block
and that block is not traded against a deadline; every review names at least
one thing to cut.

### 3. Data-driven throughout — the board's standing requirement
Every decision cites its evidence. Which topics, in which order, at what
length, targeting which keyword — all justified by Ahrefs and Search Console
data with the tool and date attached. **Never fabricate a number.** Budget
your Ahrefs units; we have used roughly 18,000 of 400,000 this month.

### 4. Publication
Articles are delivered as publish-ready files. **The engineer's pillar pages
and ingest path are being built in parallel and are not ready yet** — do not
wait for them to start writing, but nothing publishes until they land and I
approve. Deliver files.

## Owner-level rules that bind everyone on this run

- **Every image is credited to its original source** — photographer, studio,
  vendor or venue — recorded with the asset and shown on the page, so it can
  always be traced back to its owner. We approach owners for permission; the
  credit is the courtesy that earns it and the record that finds them again.
  An image whose origin nobody can name does not publish.
- **Note the rights position:** we can prove ownership of *zero* images in
  the existing library. The 401 Real Wedding files are quarantined pending
  written licences. Do not reuse library imagery in new articles. Prefer
  original graphics — cost tables, procedure diagrams, state-by-state
  comparisons — which we own outright and which suit this cluster better than
  photography anyway.
- Everything passes `/humanizer` **after** review revision, never before.
- Religious and adat terms used correctly and explained on first use.
- Report procedure, never issue a religious ruling.
