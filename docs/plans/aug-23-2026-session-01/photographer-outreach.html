Credit is not a licence

A photographer's images are copyrighted from the moment of capture. Using them with attribution but without permission is still infringement — attribution changes the courtesy, not the legality.

Our own asset register already says this. Class V requires "a written grant naming the images, the scope and the credit wording." We currently hold zero class-V assets and 658 rows with an unknown licensor, so sourcing on credit alone would deepen a rights debt rather than replace it.

The good news: this is normally free. Photographers are usually glad to be published with a credit and a link — it is how they get booked. The ask is small. What matters is that the yes is written and names HelloKahwin.

The message

Short, specific, and asks for the three things the register needs before an image can go live.

Copy and adapt
Hi — we publish HelloKahwin (hellokahwin.com), a Malay-language wedding guide. We'd love to feature some of your work as article photography, with your name and a link back to your Instagram on every image. We're mostly after detail shots rather than portraits — hantaran trays, sirih junjung, bunga telur, pelamin, the akad table. The frames that usually don't make the client gallery. If you're happy with that, could you reply confirming: 1. which images or galleries we may use, 2. that this covers hellokahwin.com including social posts promoting those articles, and 3. exactly how you'd like to be credited. Happy to work to whatever wording you prefer.
  1. Which images. A named set or a gallery link — not "some of my work".
  2. What scope. The site, plus social posts promoting those articles.
  3. What credit. Their exact preferred wording and the link they want.

Keep the reply itself. The register field bukti_lesen is meant to hold the evidence — a forwarded email or a screenshot of the DM, stored, not paraphrased.

Ask for the detail shots, not the hero portraits

Our covers fail because they don't depict their subject. The dulang-hantaran article is about gift trays and its cover is a tight crop of two guests' torsos, with no tray in frame. A more beautiful couple portrait would not have fixed that.

The images worth asking for are trays and their arrangement, sirih junjung, bunga telur, the pelamin as a set, hantaran laid out before the ceremony, the akad table, the borang being signed. Photographers shoot these constantly — they're the establishing frames in every wedding gallery — and almost never publish them, because clients want the portraits.

Which makes the ask easier, not harder. We're asking for the frames they aren't otherwise using, and they're worth far more to us than another beautiful couple shot.

The warmest opening

The Wedding Notebook already carries photographers as vendors and has published its own 15 Best Wedding Photographers in Malaysia 2026. Those are warm relationships rather than cold approaches, and the same person owns both properties — which makes the conversation easy.

It does not make the licence transferable. A vendor relationship with TWN grants TWN nothing that carries across; anything used on HelloKahwin still needs a yes that names HelloKahwin. Same ask, much warmer opening.
