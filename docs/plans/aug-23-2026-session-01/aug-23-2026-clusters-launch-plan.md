## The short version

**26 clusters. 204 mapped article topics. Roughly 143,700 Malay searches a
month.**

We are not entering a competitive market. We are entering an empty one that
two very small retailers happen to be standing in.

The most useful thing found while preparing this plan: a bridal boutique
called **ppsignature.com, Domain Rating 4**, holds **2,263 Malaysian organic
keywords, 1,493 of them in the top three, and roughly 29,745 organic visits a
month** (Ahrefs Site Explorer, `my`, index date 2026-08-01, pulled 23 Aug
2026). That is more than nikahsatu.com and TheWeddingNotebook.com combined. It
does it with a blog attached to a dress shop.

The Phase 1 audit did not find this site. It moves the argument in a useful
direction: the reason nobody owns Malay wedding search is not that it is hard.
It is that nobody has tried properly. A DR-4 retailer beat everyone by writing
about rukun nikah and mas kahwin between product pages.

Three clusters open the programme, in this order: **mas kahwin ikut negeri**
(we already sit at position 12.9), **hantaran kahwin**, **gubahan dulang
hantaran**. All three sit inside one pillar, which is deliberate.

## 1. The keyword focus argument

### 1.1 What we are fighting for

Six characteristics decide whether a Malay keyword enters this plan. A
candidate has to clear all six.

1. **Real Malay demand.** 150+ monthly searches in Ahrefs `my`. Researched on
   Malay seeds, never translated from English results.
2. **A SERP an article can win.** Page one has to contain at least one
   editorial page, or contain nothing but Pinterest, Shopee, Facebook,
   Instagram and image packs. Both cases are winnable. A page one made of
   government registration portals or e-commerce tools is not.
3. **Difficulty at or below 12.** Almost everything in this market qualifies;
   the constraint bites on maybe four keywords out of 300.
4. **A couple, or a wedding guest, is the searcher.** Not a shopper, not a
   student, not a Raya crowd.
5. **It belongs to the wedding entity.** If ranking for it would teach Google
   we are something other than a Malay wedding publication, it is out, even
   when the volume is enormous.
6. **It joins a cluster.** No orphan keyword gets a page. If it does not sit
   with seven or more siblings under one entity, it is a section of another
   article.

cost us.

### 1.3 The two SERP shapes we are attacking

Every cluster we keep falls into one of two patterns, and the difference
matters because they need different things from us.

**Shape A: one weak editorial incumbent, everything else is social.**
A typical page one holds nikahsatu at DR 14, a Pinterest board, a Facebook
group, a TikTok, a Shopee listing and an Instagram profile. Take `hantaran
tunang` (4,700/mo, KD 0), SERP Overview `my`, 23 Aug 2026: position 3 is a
**Facebook group** taking 759 visits, position 4 is nikahsatu with 1,097,
positions 2 and 9 are Pinterest boards, 6 is TikTok, 7 is a YouTube playlist.
Six of nine slots are not articles. We win by publishing one properly
structured page.

**Shape B: no editorial page at all.**
Take `bunga telur` (1,500/mo, KD 0), SERP Overview `my`, 23 Aug 2026:
Shutterstock, Dreamstime, Lazada, Shopee, YouTube, Instagram, a Facebook
group, Wikipedia (99 visits), a DR-1 shop (176 visits) and a UPM article. The
highest-traffic organic result on the entire page earns 176 visits a month. We
win by being the first publication to take the topic seriously.

Neither shape requires domain authority. Both require finishing the cluster.

### How the pillars reinforce each other

```
                        P7  Sebelum Nikah
                  (jodoh > merisik > cincin > tunang)
                                 |
                                 v
   P1 Nikah & Undang-undang <--> P2 Hantaran & Mas Kahwin
        (the legal path)              (the adat path)
                 |                          |
                 +------------+-------------+
                              v
              P5 Pelamin, Kad & Cenderahati
                              |
              +---------------+---------------+
              v               v               v
     P4 Busana       P6 Venue & Kos      P3 Ucapan & Doa
                                          (guest audience)
```

Four load-bearing joins, and the reason each exists:

- **P7 into P2** is the strongest. A couple researching `cincin tunang` is
  weeks away from researching `hantaran tunang`. Same person, in sequence. P7
  feeds P2, and P2 is where we already have pages.
- **P7 into P1** is the second. `merisik` and `bertunang` lead directly into
  `borang nikah` and `rukun nikah`. Whoever owns the beginning of the journey
  gets a free run at the middle of it.
- **P1 and P2** are the pair a couple holds open in two browser tabs. Mas
  kahwin is a legal minimum and an adat negotiation at the same time; the
  rukun nikah page and the mas kahwin page each answer half of what the
  other's reader needs.
- **P3 stands slightly apart on purpose.** Its audience is guests, not
  couples, so it converts to nothing directly. It is worth roughly 22,700
  searches a month at difficulty 0, it is nikahsatu's single largest asset,
  and the authority it earns carries across every other pillar. It links down
  into P5 at `hadiah kahwin` and `doorgift`, where guest and couple meet.

Linking rules are unchanged from the framework: the pillar links down to every
article, every article links back up, siblings link sideways two to four times
inside their own cluster, nothing links across pillars except through the
pillar pages, anchors are the target Malay entity phrase, and nothing
publishes without an inbound editorial link. No orphans.

### What changed from Phase 1

The Phase 1 framework tables hold **25** clusters, not the 21 recorded in the
Phase 1 completion log. That was a miscount on my side and this document
supersedes it.

Six Phase 1 clusters are **merged away** because they failed the eight-article
floor and amounted to coverage theatre rather than clusters: kursus kahwin
folded into pre-nikah screening; the ulang tahun and pantun clusters combined;
the four separate baju, songket, inai and solekan clusters became two; the
venue budget and checklist clusters became one.

Seven clusters are **added**: five that make up the entirely new P7, one for
wedding MC scripts, and one that promotes doorgift and bunga telur out of the
venue pillar where they never belonged.

Net 25 to 26, but the shape moved considerably.

#### C1.1 Borang & pendaftaran nikah

**Audience need:** a couple who has decided to marry and now has to deal with
the pejabat agama, in their own state, without getting it wrong twice.

**Head keyword:** `borang nikah`, 2,700/mo, KD 0, informational-transactional.

**The data argument.** Page one is government and will stay government:
`sppim.gov.my` (DR 43, 133,075 visits), `islam.gov.my` (DR 70),
`eqaryah.kelantan.gov.my` (DR 57, 6,320), `jainj.johor.gov.my` (DR 62),
`emunakahat.penang.gov.my` (DR 60), `malaysia.gov.my` (DR 77),
`jais.gov.my` (DR 53), `emunakahat.pahang.gov.my` (DR 56). We will not outrank
a registration portal for the query that means "take me to the registration
portal", and we should not want to.

Everything around it is available. The position-9 result block on that same
SERP holds a **Scribd document, a Facebook post by a district religious
office, an Instagram post, two `siraplimau.com` articles, `pesonapengantin.my`
and a Sarawak government PDF**: a scrapyard of half-answers sitting on a
2,700-a-month query. There is no editorial incumbent at all. One DR-0 blog,
`pheroessence.com`, surfaces with a "Prosedur Perkahwinan A-Z" page, which
tells us a competent explainer can reach this page.

The state variants are the real prize, and they are separate queries with
separate SERPs: `borang nikah selangor` (1,400/0), `sppim borang nikah`
(1,300/4), `borang nikah kelantan` (800/3), `borang nikah johor` (700/5),
`borang nikah kedah` (500/4), `borang nikah perak` (350/0). ppsignature
already sits at position 6 for `sppim perak` (1,000/mo) with a generic
registration explainer: a DR-4 boutique ranking on a state procedure query
without a state-specific page. That is where the opening is.

**Worth to us:** roughly 11,000 searches a month across the cluster, near-zero
difficulty, and the highest intent in the entire map. This is a couple weeks
away from paying for a wedding.

**Supporting keywords:** borang nikah online 2,500/5 · borang nikah selangor
1,400/0 · sppim borang nikah 1,300/4 · borang nikah kelantan 800/3 · borang
nikah johor 700/5 · isi borang nikah online 600/10 · borang nikah kedah
500/4 · borang nikah perak 350/0 · cara isi borang nikah 200/5.

**Topics (10),** all original Malay:

1. What the full marriage registration procedure looks like from first form to signed certificate, for a couple who has never done any of it.
2. How to complete the SPPIM online application step by step, including what breaks it, for a couple filling the form tonight.
3. Which documents each party must produce, and which ones differ for a divorcee, a convert or a widow, for a couple assembling a file.
4. How the Selangor process differs from the national default and what JAIS specifically requires, for a Selangor couple.
5. The same for Kelantan, where the enactment and the counter process are their own thing, for a Kelantan couple.
6. The same for Johor, including JAINJ's own portal, for a Johor couple.
7. The same for Kedah and Perlis, for a northern couple.
8. The same for Perak, for a Perak couple.
9. What happens when the bride and groom are registered in two different states, and which office leads, for a couple in the most common awkward case.
10. How long each stage actually takes, what causes rejection, and what to do when a form comes back, for a couple working to a fixed majlis date.

#### C1.2 Rukun, syarat & sah nikah

**Audience need:** a couple, and often a parent, who needs to know what makes
a marriage valid in Islam and in Malaysian practice, in plain Malay.

**Head keyword:** `rukun nikah`, 6,900/mo, KD 0, informational. The largest
single keyword in the Malay wedding space.

**The data argument.** This is the clearest evidence in the plan that domain
strength is not the barrier.

Page one, 23 Aug 2026: position 2 is **`ppsignature.com` at Domain Rating 4**,
earning **3,229 visits a month** from one article. Position 10 is
**`songketdunia.my` at Domain Rating 3**, earning **1,003**. Between them sit a
YouTube video, a Scribd presentation, a TikTok, a SlideShare deck, a Coggle
mind map, the Malay Wikipedia and an Indonesian bank's blog
(`cimbniaga.co.id`). The position-4 block holds Facebook posts, Instagram
posts, a Kompasiana blog and `sistemguruonline.my`.

Two retailers at DR 3 and DR 4 are the best answers on a 6,900-a-month query
because everybody else uploaded a PowerPoint. There is no editorial incumbent.

**Worth to us:** roughly 11,000 searches a month, all KD 0, and it is the topic
that establishes whether we are a real Malay wedding publication or a photo
blog. It is also the strongest internal-link hub in P1, since every other
article in the pillar has a reason to point at it.

**Supporting keywords:** syarat sah nikah 1,600/0 · akad nikah 1,000/0 ·
lafaz akad nikah 800/0 · lafaz taklik nikah 600/0 · rukun nikah dalam islam

**Topics (9),** all original Malay:

8. What makes a marriage invalid or voidable after the fact, and what can be repaired, for anyone who has been told something was done wrong.

## 5. Does the arithmetic support 1,500 clicks in 28 days?

The board's target is **1,500 clicks / 28 days by 21 Nov 2026**, from a
baseline of 32.

**On the demand side, comfortably yes.** The mapped set addresses roughly
143,700 Malay searches a month. 1,500 clicks is **1.04% of that**. There is no
version of this where keyword supply is the constraint.

**On the realistic side, it is tight, and it depends on things that are not
keyword research.**

Strip out the clusters where an article cannot capture the head, the
government SERPs in C1.1 and C1.3, the tool SERP in C5.2, the shopping SERP in
C4.1 and the image SERP in C4.2, and the article-winnable demand is roughly
**110,000 searches a month.**

The 90-day goal is 80 articles, which is 39% of the 204 mapped. If those 80
are the Tier 1 and Tier 2 clusters, they address roughly **55,000 searches a
month.**

To hit 1,500 clicks from 55,000 addressable, we need about **2.7% capture**,
which means roughly half of the 80 articles reaching the top ten within their
first weeks at a blended click-through of 5-6%.

That is achievable at difficulty 0-2 and it is not a promise. Three things
have to hold, and I want them stated rather than assumed:

1. **80 articles actually ship.** Production volume is the binding constraint,
   not competition. This is the same conclusion the framework reached and the
   reason two writer hires sit unapproved.
2. **Time-to-rank cooperates.** My own standing rule is that 6-12 months is the
   correct default for significant movement, and that faster is possible only
   where a DR-3 site already holds page one, which in this market it
   demonstrably does. **First movement in weeks and cluster-level results in
   8-12 weeks is a competitor-derived expectation, not a measurement of our own
   site.** 90 days is the optimistic end of my own rule and the board should
   hold it that way.
3. **The migration settles.** Every URL on the site changed on 21 Aug 2026, two
   days before this research. Search Console will be measuring a migration as
   much as a content programme for several weeks. The 30-day checkpoint is
   directional. The 60- and 90-day checkpoints are the scoreboard.

**Verdict: the maths supports the target. The calendar barely does.** If the
writers are not approved, the honest restatement is roughly 45 articles and a
proportionally lower click target, and I would rather bring that number
forward now than miss the larger one quietly in November.

One number argues for optimism, and it is the best evidence in this document.
`ppsignature.com` reached **1,493 top-three Malaysian keywords and 29,745
monthly visits at Domain Rating 4**, and `songketdunia.my` reached **505
top-three keywords and 10,107 visits at Domain Rating 3.** Neither is a media
company. Both did it by publishing useful Malay articles about nikah, adat and
hantaran. Nothing they did requires resources we lack.

## 6. Launch sequence

Depth-first, one pillar at a time, opening where a ranking signal already
exists. Six pillars opened at once is precisely the mistake thekenduri.com
made, and it cost that site roughly 95% of the traffic nikahsatu earns at
comparable domain strength.

| Weeks | Focus | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| **1-4** | **P2 complete.** C2.4 first, then C2.1, C2.3, C2.2, C2.5. 38 topics, plus the seven existing-article upgrades. | We already rank at 12.9 in C2.4 and own three articles in C2.1 and C2.2 sitting at zero impressions. Shortest distance to first traffic on the entire map. Finish the pillar before opening another. |
| **5-8** | **P7 opened.** C7.3 cincin first, then C7.1, C7.4, C7.2, C7.5. | The largest new opportunity, and it feeds P2 directly: the same reader moves from cincin tunang to hantaran tunang within weeks. Sequencing P7 immediately after P2 builds the journey in the order the reader lives it. |
| **9-11** | **P1.** C1.2 rukun first, then C1.1, C1.4, C1.3. | Largest pure-demand pool, and the two best pages on its head SERP are DR 3 and DR 4. Opens only once P7 is genuinely finished. |
| **12-13** | **P3 partial.** C3.1 and C3.2. | Timing-driven. `ucapan pengantin baru` peaks at 8,043 in December against 3,921 in April. Published in week 12 it is indexed and settling before the peak. Published in January it waits a year. |
| **Next sprint** | P5, then P6, then P4. | P5 has the least defended keyword on the map but no ranking signal to build on. P6 is defensive. P4 is high volume and low click value and goes last on purpose. |
| **Parallel, engineering** | Pillar pages, then the venue directory. | Neither needs editorial capacity. The pillar pages gate the entire linking architecture and block article one. The directory is the highest traffic-per-hour item found anywhere in this research. |

**The three clusters I would launch first, in order: C2.4 mas kahwin ikut
negeri, C2.1 hantaran kahwin, C2.3 gubahan dulang hantaran.** All three sit
inside one pillar, which is what depth-first requires. C7.3 cincin tunang is
the best blank-slate cluster to open after P2 is finished, and should not be
started before it.
