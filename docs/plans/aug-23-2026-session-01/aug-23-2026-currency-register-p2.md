## What this register is for

The company's only refresh trigger is traffic falling from a page's own peak. That
is a traffic signal, and it cannot see a fact going wrong. A page can be amended
into error by a state government and keep ranking, keep earning clicks, and get
reported internally as a success while it misinforms couples planning a wedding.

This register closes that hole for P2. It lists every claim in this pillar that can
expire, names the body that owns each one, and books the check before the claim is
published rather than after someone notices.

We already have proof the hole is real. Pahang changed its mas kahwin rate on 28
March 2024. Aggregator tables published as "2026 guides" still carry the old
RM22.50. They rank anyway, and nothing in their process could tell them otherwise.

That is the competitive argument for this cluster, written down. The incumbents at
positions 2, 6 and 9 are an Instagram post and two Facebook videos. They cannot be
refreshed at all. We book the refresh at publish time.

## How to read an entry

**Claim class** is the kind of fact, not one instance of it. **Owner** is the body
that can actually change it, not who reports it. **How it changes** is the
mechanism, and knowing it tells you where to watch. **Volatility** runs HIGH (has
changed within 3 years, or a change is signalled), MEDIUM (changes every few years),
LOW (stable for a decade or more), or MARKET (moves continuously, no authority).
**Cadence** is how often we check, and **next check** is the date. The date is the
part that makes the register work.

### CR-P2-04 · Hantaran and gubahan market price bands

| | |
|---|---|
| **Claim class** | What dulang, gubahan, sirih junjung, chocolate and hidden hantaran cost to buy, rent or commission |
| **Owner** | Nobody. This is a market, not a rule. There is no authority to cite and no gazette to watch. |
| **Appears in** | C2.3 Gubahan & dulang hantaran, all 8 topics. C2.1 Hantaran kahwin. C2.5. |
| **How it changes** | Continuously, with vendor pricing, materials and season. Prices spike around peak wedding months and Raya. |
| **Volatility** | **MARKET.** Different in kind from everything above, and it needs a different discipline. |
| **Cadence** | **Every 6 months**, and re-sampled rather than re-checked |
| **Last verified** | Not yet sampled. This cluster has not opened. |
| **Next check** | On opening C2.3 |

**The method, because a price band cannot be verified the way a gazette can.** A
band is honest only if it says where it came from. The rule for this pillar is to
sample at least five named vendors and record each price with the date and the URL
or the shop. Publish a range, never a single figure, and state the sample: "berdasarkan
tinjauan harga lima vendor pada Ogos 2026". Date-stamp the band inside the sentence,
so a reader in 2028 can see its age without checking a byline. Never present a
vendor's own marketing price as a market rate, and never round a sampled range into
a rounder-sounding one.

A price band without its sample size and date is a block, the same as an
unattributed legal claim. The failure mode is identical: a confident number a reader
cannot trace.

## Standing rules for P2

These sit above the SEO quality bar and the style guide on matters of fact.

1. Every religious or legal claim names its source and its state. No exceptions, and no house style that softens it.
2. Every expiring figure carries the date it was set, not the date we published.
3. We report, we do not rule. We record what an authority states, attributed. We do not say what mas kahwin ought to be, and we do not adjudicate between mazhab.
4. Variance is the answer, not a problem to tidy away. Six of fourteen jurisdictions set no minimum. Say so, then tell the reader what to do about it, which is to ask their Pejabat Agama Islam Daerah. That makes the honest answer useful instead of a shrug.
5. A declared gap beats a filled one. Where we could not verify, the article says we could not verify and says what the reader should do. TIDAK DISAHKAN is a publishable state. A guess is not.
6. Silence is silence. Where a source does not address anak dara versus janda, we say it does not.
7. Every image carries a credit naming its original source, whether photographer, studio, vendor or venue, shown on the page. An uncredited image blocks publication exactly as an unsourced claim does.
8. The block does not trade against the calendar. Not for a launch, not for a sprint, not for the CEO.
9. **Render graphic and poster-style PDFs. Do not extract them.** Fee posters stack a text layer over panel art, so extraction returns the figures out of reading order and reattaches them to the wrong labels. The output looks clean, which is the problem. This rule cost Sabah's luar daerah floor and a form-number passage in one day.
10. **The `last fetched` stamp belongs on the figure, not the document.** Pahang's RM45 was grade A in the morning and gone by afternoon, from a page whose own header date never moved. A document stamp proves the document was opened. Only a figure stamp proves the number was seen.
11. **A gazetted form field is cited by form number and printed title, verified against the instrument.** "The borang nikah" is not a citation. Three C2.4 articles independently mislabelled the same Perak field on 23 Aug 2026, which makes it a defect in the brief, not three writer errors.
