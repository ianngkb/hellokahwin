## Read this first: what to actually ask for

**Credit is not a licence.** A photographer's images are copyrighted from the
moment of capture. Using them with attribution and no permission is infringement
— attribution changes the courtesy, not the legality. Our own asset register
already encodes this: class `V` requires *"a written grant naming the images, the
scope and the credit wording."* We currently hold **zero** class-V assets and 658
rows with an unknown licensor, so sourcing without a grant would add to a rights
debt we already have rather than replace it.

**The good news: this is normally free.** Wedding photographers are usually glad
to be published with a credit and a link — it is how they get booked. The ask is
small. What matters is that the yes is *written* and *names HelloKahwin*.

**A short message that covers it:**

> We publish HelloKahwin (hellokahwin.com), a Malay-language wedding guide. We'd
> like to use a small number of your images as article photography, with your
> name and a link back to your Instagram or site on every image.
>
> If you're happy with that, could you reply confirming: (1) which images or
> galleries we may use, (2) that this covers use on hellokahwin.com including
> social posts promoting those articles, and (3) exactly how you want to be
> credited. Happy to work to whatever wording you prefer.

Three things that reply must contain — **which images, what scope, what credit** —
because those are the three fields the register needs before an image can go live.

## The gap this is actually filling — and it is not "prettier couples"

Our covers fail because they do not **depict their subject**. `dulang-hantaran` is
an article about gift trays and its cover is a tight crop of two guests' torsos,
with no tray in frame. That is not a production-value problem; it is a subject
problem, and better couple portraits would not fix it.

**So the images worth asking for are the DETAIL shots, not the hero portraits.**
Trays and their arrangement. Sirih junjung. Bunga telur. The pelamin as a set.
Hantaran laid out before the ceremony. Rings, the akad table, the borang being
signed. Photographers shoot these constantly — they are the establishing and
filler frames in every wedding gallery — and they almost never publish them,
because clients want the portraits.

That makes the ask easier, not harder: we are asking for the frames they are not
otherwise using, and the images are far more valuable to us than another
beautiful couple shot.

**Worth saying in the outreach:** *"we're mostly after detail shots — hantaran
trays, sirih junjung, pelamin, the akad table — rather than portraits."*

## What happens on our side once a yes arrives

Nothing needs building first — the pipeline already exists and has never been
used at class V.

1. Register the grant: `license_class: V`, `licensor_name`, `bukti_lesen`
   (the reply itself), `skop_lesen`, `tarikh_geran`, and the agreed `credit` and
   `credit_url` wording.
2. Ingest as normal. Every image already renders a visible credit line — that
   part shipped in Sprint 01 and is verified across 145 live images.
3. The cover-selection standard (CONT-09) decides *which* frame goes on a cover;
   this list only changes what pool it is chosen from.

**Retention note:** keep the reply. `bukti_lesen` is meant to hold the evidence,
not a description of it — a forwarded email or a screenshot of the DM, stored,
not paraphrased.
