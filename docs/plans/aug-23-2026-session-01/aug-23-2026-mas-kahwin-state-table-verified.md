# Verified state table: minimum mas kahwin, all 14 jurisdictions

**Owner:** Editorial Verification Lead
**Verified:** 23 August 2026. Every source in this table was fetched on that date.
**Pillar:** P2 Hantaran & Mas Kahwin · **Cluster:** C2.4 Mas kahwin ikut negeri
**Companion:** `aug-23-2026-currency-register-p2.md`

This table is the evidence base for every mas kahwin claim HelloKahwin publishes.
The writer works from this file, not from search results, and not from any of the
sites currently ranking for the keyword.

## 1. The headline finding, before the table

The list that dominates page one of Google is wrong, and it is wrong the same way
everywhere, because it is one list being copied.

Every ranking aggregator (ecentral.my, mingguanwanita.my, ppsignature.com,
cikgudigital.my, jomexplore.io, portalkahwin.com, kahwinstudio.com, plus the
Threads and Facebook posts holding positions 2, 6 and 9) carries the same figures.
Chasing each figure back to its source, three of them have no official backing
anywhere:

| Circulating claim | What the primary sources actually show |
|---|---|
| Perak RM101 | No Perak government source states any figure. The 2013 Perak gazette on Islamic family law forms and fees fixes no mas kahwin amount. The one official cross-state table lists Perak under "Tiada ketetapan". |
| Pulau Pinang RM24 (janda RM22) | No Penang government source states any figure. Not in the 2004 state enactment, not on JHEAIPP, not in the e-Munakahat system. Listed under "Tiada ketetapan". |
| Kedah RM22.50 | No Kedah government source states any figure. RM22.50 is Johor's number. It looks like a copy error that spread. Listed under "Tiada ketetapan". |
| Wilayah Persekutuan RM300 dara / RM150 janda, plus a separate "Labuan RM80" row | RM300 is correct and well sourced. The RM150-for-janda figure and the separate Labuan row do not exist in any primary source. JAWI states a single floor for the whole Federal Territory. |

One more, in our favour rather than against it. Pahang's rate changed on 28 March
2024, from RM22.50 to RM100. Several of the tables still ranking as "2026" guides
carry the old figure. That is the staleness this cluster exists to beat.

The editorial consequence is that our accuracy advantage here is not marginal.
Getting the "tiada ketetapan" states right, and refusing to print RM101, RM24 and
RM22.50 for Perak, Penang and Kedah, is the clearest way this page outranks what is
there now on merit.

## 2. Source grades used in this table

**Grade A, official and current and state-specific.** A `.gov.my` page or state
gazette belonging to that state's own authority, with no evidence of supersession.
Publishable as fact with citation.

**Grade B, state-specific and dated.** A dated report from a real news organisation
quoting the authority, or an official document old enough that its date must be
disclosed. Publishable with the date attached.

**Grade C, cross-state table only.** Rests solely on the JAKIM MBKPI training module
described in §3, which is 2019/2020 vintage and proven stale on at least one state.
Not publishable as a current figure without hedging.

## 4. The table

### Grade A: official, current, state-specific

#### SELANGOR, RM300

- **Figure:** RM300.00 minimum, the same for anak dara and janda, no maximum.
- **Verbatim:** "Menetapkan had minima kadar nilai Maskahwin sebanyak RM 300.00 bagi anak dara atau janda tanpa ada had maksima. Sekiranya terdapat wang hantaran daripada pihak lelaki, ianya dikira sebagai Maskahwin."
- **Source:** Gazetted fatwa. *Warta Kerajaan Negeri Selangor*, Jil. 63 No. 3, 4hb Februari 2010, Tambahan No. 3 Perundangan, Sel. P.U. 3. Fatwa under s.47, Enakmen Pentadbiran Agama Islam (Negeri Selangor) 2003. Ref [mais/pm/buu/01-3/007/7-1; PU. Sel. Agm. 0007. Jld. 2]. Signed Dato' Setia Haji Mohd Tamyes bin Abd Wahid, Pengerusi Jawatankuasa Fatwa Selangor.
- **URL:** https://www.muftiselangor.gov.my/2023/10/28/hukum-penetapan-kadar-maskahwin-bagi-negeri-selangor/ (gazette served as two page images; both downloaded and read directly)
- **Fatwa dated:** 31 Disember 2009. **In force:** 1 Januari 2010.
- **Accessed:** 23 Aug 2026.
- **Note for the writer:** the gazette says wang hantaran from the man counts as maskahwin. That is a substantive rule, it is quotable, and most competing pages get it wrong. Attribute it to the gazette and do not generalise it to other states.
- **Prior rate, for the history section:** RM80 belum berkahwin, RM40 janda, per mStar, 10 Dis 2009 (https://www.mstar.com.my/lokal/semasa/2009/12/10/mas-kahwin-selangor-dinaik-kepada-rm300). NEWS grade.

#### WILAYAH PERSEKUTUAN (Kuala Lumpur, Putrajaya, Labuan), RM300

- **Set by:** MAIWP, sidang mesyuarat kali ke-162, 22 Februari 2023. Perkenan KDYMM SPB YDPA Al-Sultan Abdullah Ri'ayatuddin Al-Mustafa Billah Shah, 19 Mei 2023. Kuat kuasa 1 Oktober 2023.

- **Do not publish RM45** until it is found in a gazetted Kaedah-Kaedah (Borang dan Fi) for Pahang or
  back on a live JAIP page. Cut from A6 on 23 Aug 2026. Logged as **CR-P2-08**.

#### JOHOR, RM22.50. TIDAK DISAHKAN sebagai kadar semasa.

- **Figure found:** RM22.50, single rate, no anak dara or janda split.
- **Sources:** MBKPI v4 (2019/2020), page 18. Plus the Johor MBKPPI V4 2022 course module, slides 37 and 38, which states "Kadar mas kahwin Negeri Johor ialah RM 22.50". That document is hosted on `kursuskahwinjohorofficial.com`, not a `.gov.my` host, so it grades SECONDARY. Its TLS certificate has also expired.
- **Historical provenance (academic, UiTM, peer-reviewed):** *Ahkam Syar'iyyah Johor 1935, perkara 309*, mahar for the whole state set at *sekati perak* (600g), equivalent to RM22.50. https://jcis.uitm.edu.my/journal/volume7/issue1/10.%20Mas%20Kahwin%20di%20Pahang%20Kiraan%20Semula%20Nilai%20Semasa.pdf
- **What we could not establish:** no Johor government page states the figure. JAINJ's site search returns nothing for "mas kahwin". No Johor state gazette instrument located. The Johor Munakahat fatwa list contains no mas kahwin fatwa.

#### SABAH, RM100. TIDAK DISAHKAN sebagai kadar semasa.

- **Figure found:** RM100, in the 2019/2020 MBKPI table only. No anak dara or janda split shown.
- **Official negative evidence:** Enakmen Undang-Undang Keluarga Islam 2004 (Sabah), No. 8 of 2004, s.21 prescribes no amount. It requires only that the Registrar "ascertain and record the value and other particulars of the mas kahwin". https://sagc.sabah.gov.my/sites/default/files/law/Islamic%20Family%20Law%20Enactment%202004.pdf (Sabah State Attorney-General's Chambers). Assent 30 Aug 2004, in force 1 Jun 2005.
- **What we could not establish:** JHEAINS Sabah, MUIS Sabah and the ekahwin.sabah.gov.my portal were all checked. None publishes a rate. MUIS site search returns "Your search yielded no results." No Sabah news report found.
- **Sources disagree:** aggregators variously claim RM100, RM200 dara with RM100 janda, and RM80. None cites anything.
- **Fees (OFFICIAL). CORRECTED 23 Aug 2026 — the morning pass got the luar daerah floor wrong.**
  Source: `https://ekahwin.sabah.gov.my/assets/images/kadar%20fii.pdf` (475,400 bytes, 2 pages).
  **Read from a 170dpi render, not from extracted text.** The file is poster-style and stacks
  its price chips over the panel art, so `pdftotext` returns the figures out of reading order and
  attaches them to the wrong category. The morning pass reported *"luar daerah rates run RM100 to
  RM250"*. The floor is **RM80**, and there are **two distinct luar daerah categories**, not one band.

  Pakej 1 = kebenaran berkahwin + pendaftaran perkahwinan & surat perakuan nikah + sijil nikah
  digital. Pakej 2 = the same four items plus Kad Perakuan Nikah (2 unit).

  | Kategori | Pakej | Warganegara | Salah seorang / pasangan bukan warganegara |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1. Perkahwinan sama daerah | Pakej 1 | RM50 | RM100 |
  | 1. Perkahwinan sama daerah | Pakej 2 | RM100 | RM150 |
  | 2. Luar daerah (salah seorang berlainan daerah) | Pakej 1 | **RM80** | RM150 |
  | 2. Luar daerah (salah seorang berlainan daerah) | Pakej 2 | RM130 | RM200 |
  | 3. Luar daerah (pasangan berlainan daerah) | Pakej 1 | RM110 | RM200 |
  | 3. Luar daerah (pasangan berlainan daerah) | Pakej 2 | RM160 | **RM250** |

  Page 2 carries three further categories the morning pass did not record at all, all Pakej 1 only,
  covering kebenaran to marry abroad, with registration applied for separately:
  category 4 perkahwinan ke luar negeri/negara (seorang), 1 unit, RM30 / RM50;
  category 5 (pasangan sama daerah), 2 unit, RM60 / RM100;
  category 6 (pasangan berlainan daerah), 2 unit, RM60 / RM100.

  The PDF's own footnote: *"Kadar fi yang dipaparkan tidak termasuk bayaran kepada Jurunikah dan
  saksi. Bayaran kepada Jurunikah dan saksi perlu dibuat pada hari pernikahan."* Quote it whenever
  the schedule is quoted, or a reader budgets short.

  Kad Nikah RM55 for two cards, https://jheains.sabah.gov.my/soalan-lazim

  *Rendered and read 23 Aug 2026. Any refresh re-renders; it does not re-extract.*

#### KELANTAN, no fixed rate found, and no official statement either way

#### PERAK, no fixed rate

- Listed under "Tiada ketetapan" in the 2019/2020 MBKPI table.
- **Official negative evidence:** *Warta Kerajaan Negeri Perak*, Jil. 66 Bil. 11, Pk. P.U. 30, 1hb Jun 2013, "Kaedah-Kaedah Undang-Undang Keluarga Islam (Borang dan Fi) 2013". Checked in full: all 20 occurrences of "mas kahwin" across its 84 pages are blank fill-in fields on forms. It fixes no amount. **CORRECTED 23 Aug 2026:** this entry previously illustrated the field as `"Mas kahwin : ………… Tunai / Hutang"`, which is not the shape of most of them and belongs to one specific form. ## 5c. Two method rules, both bought with a block today

**1. If a fee or rate source is a graphic or poster-style PDF, render it and read the
render.** Text extraction is not sufficient and is not evidence. These files stack a
text layer over panel art, and extraction returns the figures out of reading order,
silently reattaching them to the wrong label. It cost us the Sabah luar daerah floor
(RM100 printed where the source says RM80) and it cost a sibling article a passage
built on a form number that the render disproved. The signal to watch for: a document
whose extracted text has numbers and labels in the wrong sequence, or whose page count
does not match how the document reads. Render it rather than guess.

**2. A `last fetched` stamp belongs on each figure, not on each document.** Pahang's
RM45 was grade A at breakfast and gone by mid-afternoon, on a document whose header
date had not moved. A per-document stamp says the document was checked; a per-figure
stamp says *this number* was seen. Only the second one catches a figure that quietly
left a page. Every figure added to this table from here carries its own date.

## 6. Standing instructions to the writer

1. Never print RM101 for Perak, RM24 for Pulau Pinang, or RM22.50 for Kedah. They are unsourced. If a draft contains them, the review board blocks it.
2. Never print "WP janda RM150" or a separate "Labuan RM80" row. Both are aggregator inventions.
3. Never write that Pahang's RM100 comes from s.21(1) of the enactment. The section fixes no figure.
4. Where a state sets no rate, say so as a finding rather than a gap. The honest sentence is that the state does not fix a minimum and the amount is settled between the parties, then point the reader at their Pejabat Agama Islam Daerah. That is more useful than a number, and it is true.
5. Where a source is silent on anak dara versus janda, report it as silent. Only Negeri Sembilan currently has a sourced split. Do not carry that split across to other states.
6. Attach the date to every figure. "RM300, set by MAIWP on 22 February 2023 and in force since 1 October 2023" beats "RM300", and it is the whole reason we can outrank a screenshot.
7. Report, never rule. We record what each authority states. We do not say what mas kahwin should be, and we do not resolve fiqh disagreements.
8. Never print RM45 as a Pahang registration fee. It was in this table at grade A on the morning of 23 Aug 2026 and could not be re-sourced that afternoon. Pahang's only live fee today is RM15 for the Permohonan Kebenaran Berkahwin.
9. Never print Sabah's luar daerah fee as a single RM100–RM250 band. The source keeps two separate luar daerah categories and the floor is RM80. Print the table in §4 or print nothing.
10. Never quote a gazetted form field without naming the form by **number and printed title**. A field quoted as "the borang nikah" is a block. Three articles independently mislabelled Perak's Borang 7 field that way on 23 Aug 2026, which makes it a brief defect rather than three writer errors.
