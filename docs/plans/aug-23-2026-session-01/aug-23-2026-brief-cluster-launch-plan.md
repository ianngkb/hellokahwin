### 1. Cluster count

You mapped 21 clusters. **Assess whether the data supports more** — the owner
asked for as many as genuinely make sense, and P4/P6 look thinner than the
demand may warrant. Add clusters where the evidence supports them, and state
the final count with justification. Do not pad to hit a number; a cluster
that fails the test should be named as rejected, with the reason.

### 2. Per cluster

For every cluster in the final set:

- **Cluster name** (Malay) and the audience need it serves.
- **Head keyword** with volume, difficulty and intent (Ahrefs `my`, date).
- **The data argument for why we should own it.** This is the part currently
  missing and the part the board most wants. Not "culturally core" — the
  evidence: who ranks on page one today and how weakly (DR, page type,
  whether it is a forum/marketplace/Facebook post we can beat), whether an
  editorial incumbent exists at all, seasonality if the data shows it, and
  what the cluster is worth to HelloKahwin. Cite what you saw in the SERP.
- **Supporting keywords** with volume and difficulty (you have these).
- **TOPICS — the core ask.** For each cluster, list the article topics that
  would make it complete: **one line each, stating what question the article
  answers and for whom.** Not titles, not slugs, not outlines — topics. This
  is what turns a keyword list into a plan a writer can be briefed against
  later.
- **Priority tier** and where it sits in the launch sequence.
- **Supply lever** per topic where obvious (translate-and-localize from TWN
  vs original Malay) — noting your finding that the translation lever is
  weaker than assumed.

### 3. Across the whole set

- **The keyword focus argument**: which keywords we are choosing to fight for
  and — explicitly — **why not the ones you excluded**. Name the rejects and
  the data reason (too hard, too thin, wrong intent, wrong audience, wrong
  business fit). The board wants to see the discipline, not just the picks.
- **The pillar map**: how clusters interlink and which reinforce each other.
- **Total addressable monthly search volume** across the final set, so the
  board can sanity-check it against the 1,500 clicks/28d target — and say
  plainly whether the maths supports that target or not.
- **Launch sequence** with reasoning (you have this — carry it forward and
  extend it to any clusters you add).
