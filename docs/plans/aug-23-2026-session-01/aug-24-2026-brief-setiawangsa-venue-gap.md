## What I found, and why I am handing it to you rather than acting on it

Pulling GSC for the post-migration window (21–23 Aug 2026, property
`https://hellokahwin.com/`), two queries stood out:

| Query | Impressions (3 days) | Position | Clicks |
|---|---|---|---|
| `dewan komuniti setiawangsa` | 25 | 8.9 | **0** |
| `pusat komuniti setiawangsa` | 21 | 9.5 | **0** |
| `pusat komuniti setiawangsa reviews` | 1 | 7 | 0 |
| `pusat komuniti setiawangsa photos` | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| `dewan perdana keramat mall` | 1 | 13 | 0 |
| `dewan keramat` | 2 | 4.5 | 0 |

Forty-six impressions in three days on the two head terms, page one, **zero
clicks**. They are landing on `/dewan-kahwin/` — a general listicle sitting at
position 9.4 with 132 impressions and 5 clicks across the same window.

This is not a new keyword bet. It is demand we already rank for, from an
audience with an unusually specific intent — someone typing a hall's name is
much further down the funnel than someone typing "dewan kahwin murah" — and we
are converting none of it. That combination is rare and I do not want it sitting
in a spreadsheet for a month.

**This is your call to make, not mine.** I am not going to tell you the answer
is a page per hall; that is exactly the specialist judgement I hired you for.

## What I want from you

1. **Size the real pattern.** Setiawangsa and Keramat are two names that
   surfaced in a three-day window on a site with 29 posts. Use Ahrefs (MY,
   Malay) to establish how large the venue-name query space actually is — the
   `dewan komuniti` / `dewan serbaguna` / `dewan MPKJ`-type long tail across
   Klang Valley and beyond. Volume, difficulty, and who currently owns it.
   Remember `ppsignature.com` is the incumbent that matters.
2. **Diagnose the zero CTR.** Position 9 with no clicks is either a SERP-shape
   problem (a map pack or a local panel eating the click), a title/description
   mismatch against the query, or a wrong-page problem. Look at the actual SERP
   before theorising.
3. **Decide the response and justify it.** Options that occur to me, in no
   particular order and not meant to constrain you: individual venue pages, a
   structured directory, enriching the existing listicle with per-hall detail
   and anchors, or nothing at all if the tail is too thin to be worth the
   production cost. **If your answer is "not worth it", say so and show me the
   numbers** — that is a perfectly good outcome and I would rather have it now
   than after we build a directory.
4. **Place it against the 26-cluster plan.** If this warrants work, tell me what
   it displaces or whether it runs alongside. Do not let it silently expand the
   roadmap.

## Constraints

- Malay-first keyword research. Never English results translated afterward.
- Any content this produces goes through the full production doctrine: the
  review board, primary sources, `/humanizer`, and image credit to the original
  source.
- **Venue data is a factual-accuracy trap.** Hall capacities, rental rates and
  booking procedures change and are frequently wrong on the pages currently
  ranking. If we publish this, it is from primary sources — the managing
  authority, not an aggregator. The C2.4 run found three page-one mas kahwin
  figures with no official backing anywhere; assume the same rot here.
- No fabricated metrics. If Ahrefs cannot see something, say it cannot.
