C2.4 cover graphics — eight articles, two candidate sets

Nothing here has been ingested or published. Each plate is shown whole, then as the four crops the pipeline would actually cut from it, at the focal point it would actually detect.

The decision this sheet is asking for

Two approved documents specify this cover and they do not agree, so both are rendered below.

  1. Set A — kad-tajuk, data-free. Graphic kit spec §7 plus the Editorial Review Board of 24 Ogos 2026. Skop lines, title lines and alt text are rendered exactly as approved. The board's words: "No cover in this batch carries a ringgit figure. That is a board ruling on accuracy, not a design preference, and it is not the engineer's to reopen." Its reasoning is that on these eight articles the qualification IS the fact — the true answer is usually belum disahkan or tiada ketetapan, and a cover is the one surface that strips qualification on its way into WhatsApp.
  2. Set B — the state-figure cover. The generator brief of 24 Aug 2026. Every figure taken from the article it covers, nothing carried across drafts, "tiada kadar minimum ditetapkan" rendered as a figure rather than a blank.
  3. Which is later. The board sat with the brief in front of it, quoted the brief's own tie-break — "If a draft and this table disagree, the draft wins and you tell me" — and found four of the brief's eight rows disagree with their drafts. On that reading the ruling supersedes the brief. That is the CEO's call, not the engineer's.

The spec's cover geometry does not hold — 2464 × 700 breaks the mobile cover

Spec §6.2 and §7.2 say to author the cover at 2464×700, reasoning that a 3.52:1 source is "width-constrained for every other target" and that the 4:5 crop "takes the whole image". It is the other way round: computeCropWindow makes a source wider than the target height-constrained. Below is the pipeline's own function run on a 2464×700 source with the focal point §7.2 asks for (0.5, 0.5).

crop-4x5-mobile-cover560×700 at 952,0560×7001920×2400undersized
crop-4.3x1-desktop-hero2464×700 at 0,02464×7002464×700ok

The mobile cover — the surface this audience reads on — comes out a 560×700 portrait slice through the middle of the title, under a third of its target. Both sets below are authored at 2464×3080 (4:5) instead, where that crop is the whole image and the other three come out at full target size.

Read this before deciding

  1. The framing is not ours to choose yet. The pipeline centres every crop on a detected focal point, and ingest-article.mts passes no focalPointOverride — so a flat graphic falls through to sharp's saliency, which lands where it likes. The crops shown are the real ones. processSmartCrops already accepts the override and spec §7.2 asks for {x: 0.5, y: 0.5}; wiring the article file to it is a separate, one-file change, and it is what makes either set frame predictably.
  2. The desktop hero crops twice. page.tsx drops the 3.52:1 strip into an aspect-[2.4/1] box with object-cover, showing only the centre 68% of its width — x 392…2072. The mobile cover, article card and OG image all show the full width.
  3. Two crop names lie about their ratio. crop-4.3x1-desktop-hero is 3.52:1 and crop-16x9-og is 1.905:1. Both are load-bearing R2 object keys, so neither was touched.
  4. Nothing warns about an undersized cover. The crop targets are ceilings, not floors: an undersized source is silently left small and the smaller number is written into coverImageSmartCrops. There is no minimum-dimension check anywhere in the pipeline. The exact minimum that fills all four targets is 2464×2400.
  5. The credit string differs between the two documents. The brief fixes credit: HelloKahwin; board ruling 4 and style guide §13.1 fix credit: "Grafik: HelloKahwin". Each set below carries its own document's value.
  6. A5 Perak has no figure, and that is the finding. The brief's table asks for "Perak's figure"; the draft says no minimum is set at all and the gazette carries a blank field, not a number. The only Perak number in the article is RM101, which the article exists to debunk.
  7. A3 Johor's RM22.50 is not a current minimum. The draft flags it "belum disahkan sebagai kadar semasa" and records a UiTM reading of it as a maximum.
  8. A1 and A7 disagree about Sabah and Sarawak. A1's table lists RM100 and RM120 flagged "belum disahkan"; A7 states plainly that neither state sets a minimum. Each cover follows its own article, as instructed. Someone should reconcile the two before publication.
  9. Fonts. Spec §3.2 asks for Geist and Geist Mono embedded in the rasteriser. sharp rasterises SVG through librsvg, which resolves families from the host and cannot embed a face; the only Geist files in the repo are inside Next's devtools bundle. Both sets render in the system sans stack globals.css itself declares. Embedding would mean adding satori + resvg-js.
