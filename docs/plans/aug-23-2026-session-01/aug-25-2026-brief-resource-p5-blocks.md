## Why this matters more than an ordinary correction

Our entire competitive claim on this site is that **we publish what is
verifiable and say plainly when nobody publishes a figure.** The C2.4 run found
three mas kahwin figures dominating Google's page one with no official backing
anywhere — that finding is why we can beat sites with far more authority than us.

A twelve-year-old pelamin price is exactly the rot we accuse them of. We cannot
run that argument and then quote a 2014 blog.

## What I want

**For each blocked article, one of three outcomes — all acceptable:**

1. **A properly sourced figure.** Multiple current vendors, each named, each with
   the date checked. Not one shop. Not a blog. If you quote a range, say how many
   sources it spans and when you looked.
2. **An honest negative.** *"Tiada kadar rasmi diterbitkan; harga bergantung pada
   vendor, saiz dan lokasi"* — with the reasoning a reader can act on: what
   drives the price up or down, what to ask for in a quotation, how to compare
   two quotes. **This is often the better article.** A reader planning a wedding
   needs to know how to judge a quote more than they need a number that will be
   wrong by the time they book.
3. **Cut the pricing section entirely** and let the article stand on what it can
   evidence — styles, materials, what to look for, questions to ask.

**Pick per article and tell me which and why.** I am not asking you to find a
number that does not exist.
