## 2. What changed between the approved plan and these briefs, and why

The approved cluster plan specified eight topics on the assumption that each
state article would carry a figure. **The verification pass found that six of
fourteen jurisdictions fix no minimum mas kahwin at all**, and that three of the
figures on page one of Google are unsourced inventions.

That is not a problem for the cluster. It is the cluster's competitive weapon,
and it changes the shape of five briefs. Each state article now carries:

- the figure **or the sourced absence of one**, with its date and authority, and
- **the state's official fee schedule** — kebenaran berkahwin, pendaftaran,
  kad nikah, upah jurunikah, saksi, kursus — which the verification pass
  captured at OFFICIAL grade for most states, and which **no competitor
  publishes alongside mas kahwin at all**, and
- what actually happens at the Pejabat Agama Islam Daerah.

A state article that says "this state fixes no minimum, here is what it *does*
fix, here is what you will pay and what to bring" is more useful than a number,
and it is true. That is the trade this cluster is built on.

## 3. Rules binding all eight briefs

### 3.1 The do-not-print list (verification, blocking)

These appear in the first page of search results for almost every query in this
cluster. A draft containing any of them is **blocked at the review board**, and
the block is not traded against a deadline.

| Never print | Why |
|---|---|
| Perak **RM101** | No Perak government source states any figure. Official cross-state table: "Tiada ketetapan". |
| Pulau Pinang **RM24** (or janda RM22) | Same. No source anywhere. |
| Kedah **RM22.50** | Same. RM22.50 is Johor's number, copied across. |
| Wilayah Persekutuan **"janda RM150"** | Invention. JAWI states one floor for the whole territory, no status split. |
| A separate **"Labuan RM80"** row | Invention. Labuan sits inside the RM300 Federal Territory floor. |
| Melaka **"janda RM80"** | One search snippet, no source. |
| Terengganu **"RM300 dara / RM100 janda"** | Contradicted on the record by the state's own Pesuruhjaya, Utusan, 29 Mac 2024. |
| Sarawak **"janda RM100"** | No source at all. |
| "Pahang's RM100 comes from **s.21(1)** of the enactment" | False attribution. s.21 fixes no figure; it only obliges the Registrar to record the value. Attribute to the Sultan's titah, 28 Mac 2024, reported by RTM. |

### 3.2 How to handle a TIDAK DISAHKAN figure (Johor, Sabah, Sarawak)

House treatment, decided here so eight articles do not each invent one:

- Give the figure that exists, and **say in the same breath how old the source
  is and that no state authority currently publishes it.**
- Never write it as a bare current fact.
- Point the reader at the state's own office as the way to close it, with the
  actual contact route.
- Do not hedge into uselessness either. "Angka yang beredar ialah RM22.50, dan
  sumber terbaik yang kami temui bertarikh 2019/2020" gives the reader
  something. "Tidak jelas" gives them nothing.

### 3.3 How to handle a state that fixes no rate

Say it as a **finding, not a gap**. The honest sentence is that the state does
not fix a minimum, the amount is settled between the parties, and the reader's
Pejabat Agama Islam Daerah records it. Then give them what the state *does*
fix: the fee schedule. Empty cells are banned. "Tiada kadar minimum ditetapkan"
is an answer.

Where a source is **silent** on anak dara versus janda, report it as silent.
**Only Negeri Sembilan currently has a sourced split.** Do not carry that split
across to other states.

### 3.4 Report, never rule

Owner-level. We record what each authority states. We never say what mas kahwin
*should* be and we never resolve a fiqh disagreement. Per the Managing Editor's
attribution rule: any sentence carrying *wajib, haram, sunat, makruh, harus,
sah, tidak sah* must name its authority, and its date if the claim can expire,
in the same sentence or the next one. That rule is grep-able, and it will be
grepped at QC.

### 3.8 Sourcing discipline — added 23 Aug 2026, in force from the next brief

Five rules, each earned by something a review board caught in C2.4. They live
here rather than in a board record because catching the same defect three times
downstream means the brief is wrong, not the writer.

**S1. A gazette form field is quoted only with its form number, its printed
title, and the section it is prescribed under, reproduced exactly as printed
rather than tidied. Where the same instrument renders the field differently
across forms, the draft quotes one form and says which. A form is described by
what it is for, taken from its own printed title, never by a general label such
as "borang nikah".**

Four articles carried the same mislabelled Perak form. The third part of that
rule is the one that mattered: naming an overseas-registration application a
`borang nikah` is what made the sentence **false** rather than merely imprecise,
because a reader who checks Perak's actual nikah form finds no such field. And
trimming an official quotation for house style is a small forgery. Tidying a
field while relabelling the form is the same failure wearing a different coat.

**S2. A source note may not record a verification with no retrievable artefact
behind it.** A2's worst block was a full-text search recorded as done, at Grade
A, on the one source with no URL. It had not been run, or had been run on a
partial document, and an argument was built on the result. If a check cannot be
re-run from what the note records, the check did not happen.

**S3. A brief names the source URLs it expects to be used.** The verification
seat exhausted its 200-call WebSearch budget on **discovery** — finding sources
whose location nobody had recorded — and finished only because direct fetches
still worked. One wrong URL in a brief cost a paged index search to correct.
Naming known URLs up front turns verification into fetch-work, which is cheaper
and which is also the upstream fix for the wrong URL reaching the board at all.
Verified source texts from this cluster are cached and should be pointed at
rather than re-fetched.

**S4. Where a fee or rate source is a graphic or poster-style PDF, the source
step renders it and reads the render.** Text extraction is not sufficient.
Two separate Stage 2 passes read stacked-text-layer PDFs by extraction; one
produced interleaved digits and hid a wrong floor (Sabah RM80 read as RM100).
**Where a writer's re-read disagrees with the verified table, that escalates at
Stage 2 rather than becoming a widened band in the draft. A band is how a draft
hides an unresolved read.**

**S5. A brief may not assert a checkable claim about a competitor's page, a
source's contents, or a document nobody has opened.** Three variants of this hit
C2.4 and all three were mine: a competitor-state claim that live checking
falsified, a source summary that had hardened into a fact, and an image note
describing a document's contents that turned out true for one form and false for
another. **In this cluster specifically: no sentence about what other pages
show, at all.** The finding does not need a competitor to be interesting, and a
dated competitor claim is a maintenance liability on a page that already carries
an annual refresh.

### A6 — Mas kahwin Pahang & Negeri Sembilan

**Priority 3.** Best-sourced article in the cluster. Both states are Grade A.

- **Target keywords:** `mas kahwin pahang` **700/mo** + `mas kahwin negeri sembilan` **700/mo**, both KD 0 (Ahrefs, `my`, 23 Aug 2026). **1,400/mo combined.**
- **Template:** Type 1 PANDUAN. 1,100 to 1,500 words.
- **Slug:** `mas-kahwin-pahang-negeri-sembilan`

**The thesis pairing them:** these are the two states that fix a rate *and*
publish it properly, and each shows a different mechanism — **Pahang by the
Sultan's titah, Negeri Sembilan by a departmental schedule.** Pahang also
carries the freshest change in the country and Negeri Sembilan the only sourced

**Note for later, not now:** if this ranks for only one of the two states at day
45, it splits into two articles. That is a measurement question, not a drafting
one.

**Pahang:** RM100, raised from RM22.50 by titah KDYMM Sultan Pahang, **28 Mac
2024**, reported by RTM the same day and by the state portal on 29 Mac. **Never
attribute it to s.21(1)**: the section fixes no figure. Effective date is not
stated on any source and no gazette was located; say so rather than inventing
one. Several pages still ranking as "2026 guides" carry the old RM22.50, and

### A7 — Mas kahwin Sabah & Sarawak

**Priority 8.** Lowest volume, weakest data. Last for both reasons.

- **Target keywords:** `mas kahwin sabah` **350/mo** + `mas kahwin sarawak` **350/mo**, both KD 0 (Ahrefs, `my`, 23 Aug 2026). **700/mo combined.**
- **Template:** Type 1 PANDUAN. 900 to 1,200 words.
- **Slug:** `mas-kahwin-sabah-sarawak`

**The thesis:** East Malaysia runs on separate legislation, Sabah's Enakmen
Undang-Undang Keluarga Islam 2004 and Sarawak's Ordinan 2001, Cap. 43, and
**neither fixes an amount.** Both only require the Registrar to record the
value. Peninsular assumptions do not transfer, and that is the point of the
page.

**Both figures are TIDAK DISAHKAN** and get §3.2 treatment. Sabah's RM100 rests
on the 2019/2020 module alone. **Sarawak's RM120 traces to a single Utusan quote
from 14 Disember 2009, seventeen years old, which survives only as a blog
reproduction because the Utusan original is gone.** Write that plainly. It is
more informative than the confident number above us.

**The compensating substance is the fees, and they are OFFICIAL for both
states.** Sabah's ekahwin schedule: Pakej 1 RM50 (warganegara, sama daerah) or
RM100 (bukan warganegara); Pakej 2 adds two Kad Perakuan Nikah at RM100/RM150;
luar daerah RM100 to RM250; jurunikah and saksi not included; Kad Nikah RM55 for
two. Sarawak: permohonan kebenaran berkahwin RM10, sijil perkahwinan RM15 (two
sources agree).

**Do not print:** Sarawak "janda RM100". Sabah's aggregator variants (RM200/RM100, RM80).

**Questions:**
1. How much is mas kahwin in Sabah? In Sarawak?
2. Why is East Malaysia different?
3. Are the circulating figures reliable? (no, and here is exactly why)
4. What will the marriage cost me in each state?
5. Who confirms the figure?

**Internal links out:** pillar; A1; A2; A8.

**Images:** original graphic, the two enactments side by side, showing that
each requires the value be *recorded* rather than *fixed*. `Grafik: HelloKahwin`.

### A8 — Bolehkah mas kahwin melebihi kadar minimum

**Priority 4.** No expiring figures, high differentiation, and it is the piece a
competitor cannot copy without understanding the custom.

- **Target keyword:** `mas kahwin melebihi kadar minimum` — **long-tail within the cluster's 11,000/mo pool. No standalone Ahrefs volume was pulled for this exact phrase in the approved plan, and I am not inventing one.** It is briefed on approved-topic grounds (C2.4 topic 8) and on the head keyword's question set, not on a fabricated figure.
- **Template:** Type 3 SOAL-JAWAB. 800 to 1,100 words.
- **Slug:** `mas-kahwin-melebihi-kadar-minimum`

**Why it is in the cluster despite no pulled volume.** Cluster completeness is
question-shaped, and this is the question the head keyword's readers arrive
carrying: they found the minimum, and the minimum is not what their family is
discussing. Answering it is what keeps them on the site. It also directly
serves QC3: no competitor page on page one answers this.

**Questions:**
1. Is the state minimum the amount I pay, or the floor?
2. Can it be exceeded? Is there a maximum?
3. Who decides the actual figure?
4. What happens when the two families disagree?
5. Does a higher mas kahwin mean anything, religiously or socially?
6. Can it be paid later? (mas kahwin hutang)
7. What gets recorded on the marriage certificate?

**Required structure:** the direct answer in 40 to 60 words. H2 minimum versus
maximum, with Selangor's gazette quoted for "tanpa ada had maksima". H2 who
decides. H2 how families settle a figure without either side losing face. H2
mas kahwin tunai versus hutang. H2 what is recorded. Soalan Lazim.

**This is the article where the voice notes matter most.** Money between two
families, written without moralising and without status-anchoring. The Managing
Editor's banned list is binding here in both directions: **no** `bukan menjual
anak`, `jangan bebankan pihak lelaki`; **no** `nampak murah`, `jangan malu`.
Report how families actually settle it. Do not tell them what to feel.

**Report, never rule.** The religious question in Q5 is answered by citing what
authorities state, not by ruling.

**Internal links out:** pillar; A1; A2; A6.

**Images:** one original graphic, a simple decision diagram from "state
minimum" to "the figure recorded on the sijil". `Grafik: HelloKahwin`.
