## The standard

Costs, materials and vendor claims are factual and go stale. Date every figure,
source it primarily where one exists, and give an evidenced range rather than
splitting the difference between two blogs.

The C2.4 run found three figures dominating Google's page one with **no official
backing anywhere.** Bridal and decor pricing is at least as unreliable. Where
nobody publishes a real number, say that — it is a finding, not a gap.

**Do not drift into a vendor directory.** Naming vendors as examples inside a
guide is useful. A list of vendors with contact details is the thing the board
decided not to build.

## Format — non-negotiable

**One Markdown file per article with YAML front matter the parser accepts** —
exactly what you did for C6.2, which was correct.

- **No `*[IMEJ N di sini]*` markers.**
- **Name the cover you want** precisely; covers are generated from your
  description.
- **In-article images:** describe each in an `images:` entry with real Malay alt
  text. For our own graphics use `credit: HelloKahwin`, `licenseClass: G`. **Do
  not describe a photograph we do not hold rights to** — put it in your gap list
  instead.
