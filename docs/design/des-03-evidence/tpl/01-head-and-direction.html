The direction, and what survives 390 px

Direction A — Warkah, the record — was chosen by the owner on 28 Ogos 2026 on three grounds, none of them taste. Those three grounds are constraints on this document, so they are restated as constraints rather than as history.

1.1  What Warkah actually is

A publication of record, warmly printed. Not a bridal magazine. The mark carries the whitespace; the page carries the density. Whitespace here has one job and it is stated: it separates the fields of a record — state, rate, issuing authority, date checked — so they can be scanned. It is not there to signal expense.
