The empty/error distinction, and why it is two components, not one

DES-07 rule 3.5: the empty and error blocks must be visually distinguishable at a glance. .s-empty carries no colour signal — nothing is wrong, there is simply nothing here yet. .s-err opens with a 3 px --alert rule and sets its heading in --alert. A reader scanning past either should be able to tell which one it is before reading the words.
