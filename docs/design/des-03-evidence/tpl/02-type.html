2.1  The faces

A face name is a label, not a specification. On Bodoni Moda the opsz axis moves the hairline by 94% and the set width by 4.5%, so two marks carrying the same family name can be different marks. Latin coverage, checked once and closed

Bodoni Moda 2.005 carries Basic Latin 95/95, Latin-1 Supplement letters 64/64 and Latin Extended-A 128/128. Rumi Malay is plain Latin: measured across every h1, h2, h3 and title on production — all 86 article titles and all 15 category names — the site's display type sets 69 codepoints, of which exactly two are non-ASCII, and both are dashes. Not one accented letter. Diacritic coverage cannot discriminate between two shipping Latin faces here, so it is not a selection criterion. Licence and cost are.

The face is free; loading it is not

DES-09 G23 caps HTML+CSS+JS at 266,240 B a page. The article page already spends 251,692 B, leaving 14,548 B of headroom against a 21,388 B face. It does not fit until something gives the bytes back: DES-04's 6,317 B of admin-only CSS, or part of the 202,523 B of JavaScript on a page that is, on the reading path, static text and pictures. DES-05 lands one of those before the face is loaded. That is arithmetic, not an objection.

Where the drawings differ from the build, said out loud so nobody trips on it. Every screen below sets the wordmark in the live variable face at opsz 6 so the letterforms are right on screen. In the build the wordmark is the outlined SVG mark and costs zero font bytes, and the 21,388-byte subset is pinned at opsz 11 and serves the h1 and nothing else. One file, one instance, one job.

2.2  The scale is fluid, and here is why it has to be

The live article ships two h1 elements carrying identical text — text-[1.75rem] and text-[2.5rem], one hidden per breakpoint, both in the DOM. Re-verified in the delivered HTML of /artikel/hantaran-mas-kahwin/mas-kahwin-ikut-negeri on 28 Ogos 2026: h1 count = 2, h2 count = 1, h3 count = 13. The 0.5em average advance behind the ch column is an estimate, because the body face is whatever the reader's operating system supplies. The container widths are exact and the 68ch cap is what makes the estimate safe — above 1024 px the cap binds, not the column.

Against what ships today

The live article sets font-size:.875rem; line-height:1.5 inside max-w-7xl px-2 lg:px-4 — 14 px in up to 1,248 px, roughly 160 to 175 characters a line. .hk-measure (max-width:40rem) is defined in the served stylesheet and used zero times in the delivered HTML. Both re-verified against the served artefacts on 28 Ogos 2026.

The number I am not going to round up

At 360 px the measure is about 39ch, under the 45ch comfort floor. Every remedy is worse. Smaller type raises the character count by making the type harder to read; narrower gutters cost the 328 px column that takes title truncation from 66% to zero. On a 360 px screen the measure is set by the screen, and that is stated rather than dressed up.

2.4  What the display face may set, and what it may not

May

The wordmark. The article h1. The category h1. The 404's h1. That is the complete list: four elements across four surfaces.

May not

h2, h3, decks, pull quotes, table headers, numerals, buttons, nav, captions, credits, or anything set below 24 px. A Didone at small sizes is a hairline problem wearing a style. There is no italic file, no bold file and no second family — asking for one is a change request, not an implementation detail.
