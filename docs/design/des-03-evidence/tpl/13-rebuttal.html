The strongest argument against this, answered

The objection, stated as strongly as it can be

A record-first layout with a library that only trusts four photographs at scale will read as a government gazette, not a wedding publication. Direction B put a full-bleed photograph on every card because a wedding site's job is to make a reader feel something before it informs them. Warkah leads with a rate table. That is the argument, and it does not go away because the owner already chose A.

Three things are true at once, and none of them is a matter of taste.

The intent ground is a fact about this site, not a preference

The highest-impression page on hellokahwin.com is a rate table. A reader arriving from mas kahwin Perak is not browsing for inspiration; DES-06 measured 84.3% of arrivals hunting a named thing. A layout optimised for the feeling of scrolling a gallery serves the reader B was built for, and that is not the reader who is actually here in the numbers we have.

The inventory ground is not a design failure to route around

Four of eleven library photographs survive enlargement. A layout that needs eleven of eleven to look considered is a photography commission wearing a layout costume — DES-02's finding, restated in §6. Warkah's five-device identity in §1.2 is deliberately checkable with the photograph covered, which is the property that lets it ship against the library that exists rather than the one a full-bleed design would require.

Where the objection is right, and what this document does about it

It is right that a page of nothing but fields and rules can go cold. That is why the credit line is designed rather than appended (§1.2's fifth device), why every photograph that does appear runs at its real crop rather than a stock thumbnail, and why §6 spends real space defending which photographs earn a large placement rather than papering over the gap with a placeholder. The warmth is supposed to come from specificity — the real rate, the real state, the real date it was checked — not from a photograph doing the work the text should be doing. Whether that reads as warm rather than clinical is exactly the kind of judgement a canvas review answers and a written rebuttal cannot; DES-02's rendered comps are the evidence for that half of the argument, and this document does not re-litigate what the owner already decided from them.
