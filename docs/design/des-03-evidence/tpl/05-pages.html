How to read the frames

Phone frames are exactly 360 px — the narrowest supported viewport, and the one DES-07 measured every failure at. Desktop frames are exactly 1200 px. Both scroll inside their own rail rather than resizing the page, so a measurement taken off this document is the measurement specified. Light and dark are drawn side by side and do not follow your theme: the site ships light only, and this document has to show both regardless. The frames set the scale's endpoint values directly rather than computing the clamp, because a 360 px box inside a wide page is not a 360 px viewport. The clamps themselves are in §2.2.

5.1  Article — the record above the fold

The composition is the direction. Eyebrow, headline, deck, then the Rekod panel, and the reader searching mas kahwin Perak has the answer before the photograph loads. On desktop the panel is the 300 px rail; on a phone it is a full-width block in the same place in the reading order. That is the identity surviving 390 px, and it survives 360 px too.

5.3  Homepage — one hero, then the record of what is new

Measured on the live homepage today: thirteen items, all thirteen from Hantaran & Mas Kahwin, with the same 21-character eyebrow printed thirteen times, and the same thirteen items are still there on 01 September 2026. The specified homepage carries the diversity rule — H6, defined in §7.5, which caps any one category at ceil(N/3) of the set, forbids two consecutive items from the same category, and is evaluated by scripts/measure/check-h6.sh rather than by reading. The drawing below shows it: the hero, then rows that name different categories.

The hero drawn here is not the hero production runs, and that is the point

Production's hero today is Persiapan hantaran kahwin: jadual lapan minggu, whose cover is a wide group snapshot taken at distance. §6 disqualifies that frame class from hero scale, so the drawing runs the next eligible article instead. This is the inventory constraint doing its job in public rather than being discovered during the build: the homepage hero is selected from articles whose cover is class O or class P, and if none is available the homepage runs H3's no-hero variant rather than enlarging a frame that cannot take it. Cover class becomes an editorial selection input, not a rendering detail, and that is a change managing-editor needs to know about.
