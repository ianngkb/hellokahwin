Space, grid and breakpoints

Whitespace in Warkah has one stated job — it separates the fields of a record so they can be scanned. It is not there to signal expense. Every value below is on a 4 px base, and every one names what it separates. A scale step with no job attached is not on the scale.

The two rhythm rules that stop this drifting

One. Vertical space between sibling blocks is set by the parent's gap, never by a margin on the child. A child that carries its own margin collapses differently depending on what precedes it, which is how a rhythm becomes a set of exceptions.
Two. Section separation is a single step: --sp-8 below 1024 px, --sp-9 at and above. There is no third value, and a block that wants one is asking to be a section.

328 px is a measurement, not a preference

DES-07 ran all 86 live titles through the real card box at three widths. At the shipped 156 px two-column card, 57 of 86 are cut and the worst loses 46 of 95 characters. At 236 px, 5 are cut. At 328 px, none are — every title on the site fits in three lines and 77 of 86 fit in two. That single table decides the one-column layout, and it is why the gutter below 390 px is 16 and not 20.

The rail is not allowed to be empty

DES-02 rendered direction A and found 300 px of dead rail beside the photograph — a hole for a screen and a half. The fix is in the spec, not left to judgement: the rail carries the Rekod panel, then the section index, then the source list, in that order, and it never starts a block it cannot fill. If a page has no sources and no headings, the rail ends after the Rekod panel and the text column widens to the full 1120 px. A rail that runs out is worse than no rail.
