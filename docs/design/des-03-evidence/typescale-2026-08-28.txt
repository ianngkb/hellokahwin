Why fluid, in one number
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  The live article ships TWO <h1> elements with identical text - text-[1.75rem]
  and text-[2.5rem] - one hidden per breakpoint. Verified in the delivered HTML
  of /artikel/hantaran-mas-kahwin/mas-kahwin-ikut-negeri on 28 Ogos 2026: h1 count = 2.
  A single clamp() covers 28.00px -> 40.00px across the same range with one node.

Measure, in px, at the container widths this spec defines
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  360px phone,  16px gutters                                 body 17.00px  column  328px  = ~ 38.6ch  -> renders at 39ch
  390px phone,  20px gutters                                 body 17.05px  column  350px  = ~ 41.1ch  -> renders at 41ch
  768px tablet, 32px gutters                                 body 17.61px  column  704px  = ~ 79.9ch  -> renders at 68ch
  1024px desk,  40px gutters + 300px record rail + 48px gap  body 18.00px  column  596px  = ~ 66.2ch  -> renders at 66ch
  1200px desk,  40px gutters + 300px record rail + 64px gap  body 18.00px  column  756px  = ~ 84.0ch  -> renders at 68ch
  1440px desk,  container caps at 1200                       body 18.00px  column  756px  = ~ 84.0ch  -> renders at 68ch

  0.5em average advance is an ESTIMATE - the body face is the system stack, so the
  advance is whatever the reader's OS supplies. The container widths are exact.
  The 68ch cap is what makes the estimate safe: above 1024px the cap binds, not the column.

  328px at 360px viewport is not a taste number: DES-07 measured all 86 live titles
  in that box and 86 of 86 fit in three lines with nothing clamped. At the shipped
  156px two-column box, 57 of 86 are cut.
