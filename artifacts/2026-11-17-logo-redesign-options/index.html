Astra’s assessment

The direction is right. The execution needs discipline.

Restore the chosen wordmark

The right-hand Fashion Publication option was light, upright and restrained. Later boards introduced a heavier face and a sweeping K. The new options remove that sweep; final artwork should match the original selection’s proportions.

Let the name lead

Use HelloKahwin as the website masthead. The seal is a companion for avatars, editorial signatures and brand moments. These large seal specimens are for inspection, not a proposed header layout.

Make a small-size cut

The circle reduces the space available to HK. Test actual 16, 32 and 48px vectors. If needed, strengthen the hairlines and simplify the junction; an unframed HK can be tested at the smallest size.

Keep the identity consistent

The website already has outlined, all-capital SVG logos and a separate icon generator. The pink PNG reviewed earlier is not an adequate picture of the current system. Replacement must include those assets and their usage rules.

A separate mark from The Wedding Notebook

TWN’s actual symbol is a pair of interlocking rings with a diamond. I would keep the HK seal distinct: it suits a publication-led identity. Share editorial quality and monochrome treatments where appropriate; reusing the ring symbol would imply a much closer brand relationship.

Full design review findings

Two Astra review passes examined the original artifact: design critique and document structure. This is a design review, not a production-code verdict. The review skill helped identify drift from the approved selection and reorganise the comparison.

Recommended direction

Finding: The page recommends blending three wordmarks despite selection of Fashion Publication.

Action: Treat the right-hand Fashion Publication wordmark as the reference.

Consequence: Further refinement could lose the restraint Ian selected.

Logomark board — wordmarks

Finding: The later board restores a sweeping K absent from the selected wordmark.

Action: Use a straight, restrained K and match the selected lighter proportions.

Consequence: Approval of the seal could be mistaken for approval of different lettering.

Wedding-led variations and System card

Finding: Wedding symbols remain active recommendations after Ian returned to the original seal.

Action: Archive the wedding additions as not selected.

Consequence: The implementation could drift from the selected editorial identity.

Original Editorial Seal — HK junction

Finding: Both letters rely on a delicate curved connection.

Action: Compare the curved connection with a clearer straight H crossbar.

Consequence: HK may read as an ornament before it reads as initials.

Small applications

Finding: Enlarged concept boards do not establish favicon legibility.

Action: Test final vectors at actual 16, 32 and 48px, and at the mobile header size.

Consequence: The circle can crowd the letters and hairlines can disappear.

Monochrome specimens

Finding: The original boards lack white-on-black examples.

Action: Add reverse concepts now and matched production proofs after vector construction.

Consequence: Reversed artwork may appear heavier or lose internal detail.

Production artwork

Finding: No verified vector master exists for the selected combination.

Action: Label concepts accurately and compare final outlined vectors against the selected reference.

Consequence: A font substitution or trace could change the approved design.

Seal and wordmark hierarchy

Finding: The large seal above the name does not establish a wordmark-led website hierarchy.

Action: Use the name as masthead, the seal for avatars, and test a modest combined signature.

Consequence: A site-wide rollout could become dominated by badges.

Existing website brand registry

Finding: Existing variants permit KAHWIN alone, stacked words, warm ink, parchment and gold.

Action: Replace logo variants and logo colour rules consistently; remeasure proportions and minimum sizes.

Consequence: Old variants and measurements could survive the rollout.

Relationship to The Wedding Notebook

Finding: The earlier recommendation did not show the actual TWN mark.

Action: Show the actual interlocking-ring TWN mark and identify the distinct HK seal.

Consequence: The relationship and implications of symbol reuse remain unclear.
