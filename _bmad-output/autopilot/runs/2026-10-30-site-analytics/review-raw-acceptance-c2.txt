NOTE | src/lib/analytics/__tests__/hash.test.ts:71 | D5's check that no part of the IP appears in the hash is weak. A hex digest can never contain `.`, so the dotted-fragment loop and `not.toContain('.')` can't fail once the 64-hex test passes. Only `not.toContain(ip)` is a real check. Digit-only fragments such as `203` or `113` can't be asserted absent from a hex string anyway.
NOTE | src/lib/analytics/visitor-id.ts:166 | On the client, `decodeURIComponent(match[1])` has no try/catch. A malformed `_hk_vid` cookie such as `%zz` would throw a URIError inside `track()` or a click handler, which is exactly the failure `uuid.ts` was written to avoid. This is a robustness issue, not a spec gap.
