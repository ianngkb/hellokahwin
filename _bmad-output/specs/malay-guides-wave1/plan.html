The biggest finding isn't a new topic

HelloKahwin already has guides for rukun nikah (7,200 searches a month), ucapan pengantin baru (5,500), kursus kahwin (3,400), cincin tunang (3,200) and borang nikah (2,700). According to Ahrefs, none of them appear in Google's top 100 for their main term. Fixing these is worth about twice as much as all the new guides below. That's why it's step 1.

Step 1 result · 15 Nov 2026

Why the existing guides aren't ranking

  • Search Console has lost its owner. Google shows every account on hellokahwin.com, including the site's own, as "unverified". The verification record is gone from the site and from its domain settings. Until someone re-verifies, nobody can see ranking data, and Google can't receive sitemap updates from the site. This needs Ian: re-verify the domain in Search Console. It takes about 10 minutes at the domain provider.
  • The pages are young, and the site has almost no outside links. Most of these guides went live on 25 Aug 2026, about 12 weeks ago. Ahrefs gives hellokahwin.com a link-strength score of 0 out of 100, meaning almost no other sites link to it. Google is slow to trust new pages on a site like that for big search terms.
  • The writing is not the problem. The guides are 1,300–2,300 words, well structured, and open to Google (no blocking tags, correct page addresses).
  • Some main search terms can't realistically be won. For "borang nikah" and "kursus kahwin", Google shows government sites (SPPIM, JAWI, state religious departments) at the top. For "cincin tunang", it shows jewellery shops. Aim those guides at the longer, specific searches instead, for example by state, how-to, or "jari mana".
  • Two are winnable soon. For "rukun nikah" and "ucapan pengantin baru", the sites on top are small (link scores of 4–26).
  • Links between our own guides matter. The hantaran tunang guide ranks #1 and has 17 links from inside other articles. The guides that don't rank have only 3–6.
  • The overall direction is good. Google traffic grew from about 16 visits a week in August to about 810 a week now, with a dip in mid-October.

What to do

  • Ian re-verifies Search Console. This comes first, so everything after it can be measured.
  • Add links to these 11 guides from related HelloKahwin articles, aiming for 10–15 each. Edits only, nothing new to write.
  • Get a few real outside links. TWN's Malay-wedding articles linking to the matching HelloKahwin guide is the easiest start.
  • Re-check rankings in January, once the August pages are about 5 months old.

Deliberately skipped

  • Translation searches ("tunang in english", "kahwin in english")

Rules for writing these

  • Every "Rules" guide is checked against the official source for that state: the state religious department or court, and SPPIM for nikah forms and courses. Amounts and steps change and vary by state, and a wrong figure on a religious or legal topic damages trust quickly.
  • Each guide shows the date it was last checked, so readers know how current it is.
  • Written in natural Malay from scratch, not translated, in HelloKahwin's existing voice, then run through the humanizer check.
  • Each new guide links to its main guide (borang nikah, mas kahwin, kursus kahwin) and back, so the topic group grows together.
  • Saved as drafts for review. Nothing goes live without your approval.
